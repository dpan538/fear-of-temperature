# Source frame, identity and metadata contract

Publication eligibility remains 1 January 1988 through 21 September 2026. September is partial. Publication precision and narrower observed source times are preserved. Retrieval, Load, content-version and report timestamps are separate, and a later retrieved native body does not prove historical-version equivalence.

Newspaper quantities count complete stable article identities in the accepted regular frame. Supplement is a separate edition subframe, campus publications remain separate, and the three month-dated Poetry works remain creative works. A native HTTP response, confirmed-complete Load event, saved version or raw object is not automatically a new distinct regular article. Candidate source registry rows and acquisition adapters are not actual contributing titles. Newspaper counts use the retained original five-region denominator; additive English-region discoveries do not retrospectively enlarge it.

Social quantities distinguish native entities, changed versions, independently authored dated bodies, source/site paths, source-qualified author keys and explicitly linked named people. Prepared or profile-verified cohorts are not necessarily contributors. Two explicitly linked sites by one named author are two site paths and one known author relationship, not two independent people. Platform instances and APIs are acquisition contexts, not interchangeable distinct public communities. Technical mailing-list bodies stay retained while all new technical-list requests and Loads are zero.

Source/publisher/community country, publisher parent or access-family relation, and time-specific author self-declared geography are separate dimensions. A current declaration does not establish country for every historical body. Unknown author geography and unresolved source-country/parent relations stay explicit. A platform/server country, scene, subject, workplace, nationality, language or name cannot fill author residence. Parent metadata labels are not automatically verified independent legal publishers.

The ELT chain extracts native text, performs structural cleanup and immediately durably Loads identities and versions. Final changed-tranche checks verify declared native/SQL text hashes, declared sidecars, raw mappings, fixed publication dates, stable baseline membership and source-month reconciliation. SQL-native bodies with no declared sidecar are valid storage cases. Checks apply once to the changed tranche and reuse accepted inherited evidence; the coordinator does not scan old raw bodies or databases.

Source access, retention permission, open licensing and public redistribution are different evidence fields. The package publishes code and metadata, not third-party full bodies or raw objects. MemberPress/paywall previews, migrated teasers, recordings, title/body/date ambiguities, robots stops, HTTP restrictions and unknown permission remain distinct pending or blocked dispositions. Public reachability alone does not establish redistribution rights. Source restrictions, native/journal reserves and single-writer safeguards remain binding.

Counts, missingness, temporal peaks, source/genre/era composition and geography are descriptive structural diagnostics. They are not acquisition quotas or claims of country representativeness, archive completion, climate relevance, causal influence or fear prevalence. No semantic exclusions, artificial temporal smoothing, source deletion or corpus-wide climate/affect/fear labels are performed.
