Run from repository root with `.venv/bin/python`. `eu_acquire.py enumerate` saves each official SPARQL response body and request metadata before creating its monthly Work/date manifest. It uses deterministic `ORDER BY ?work ?date LIMIT/OFFSET` pages. Existing pages are reused only after byte-hash verification. Each month is reconciled to the independent 08 month count; mismatches stay unresolved. All 465 monthly bins from 1988-01 through the 2026-09-21 cutoff were reconciled on 26 September 2026, yielding 50,578 globally distinct Work URIs. The manifest is metadata coverage, not full-text coverage. A 403/429/access challenge or repeated transport failure stops the current run; never bypass it.

The 2024-08 bin is a verified zero for this exact COM/`act_preparatory`/English-expression query: the saved official CSV contains only its header, the independent month count is zero, and the WEMI manifest has zero enumerated Works. It does not imply that the Commission published nothing in August 2024 outside this defined series. A bounded official CELLAR diagnostic for [CELEX 52024PC0999](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:52024PC0999), dated 2024-08-09 and authored by COM/HOME, returned type `cdm:proposal_decision_implementing_ec` rather than `cdm:act_preparatory`; see the hashed response `raw/diagnostics/52024PC0999_cellar_work.csv`. This is a concrete class-boundary omission for broader COM proposals, not evidence that the saved query malfunctioned. Keep the frozen denominator unchanged and disclose the adjacent class separately if expanding source scope.
The lower 2024–2026 counts in this class may reflect a wider use of more specific document types, but this one diagnostic does not quantify the cause of the annual decline. Treat those months as coverage of the frozen class only.

`eu_wemi.py` follows each Work to English Expression, Manifestation and digital Item in official SPARQL, preserving every returned relationship and separately recording Works without digital Items. It works in hash-verified 50-Work request batches. `eu_items.py` selects one candidate Item per Work, records alternative count, downloads the original stream with SHA-256 and request evidence, and extracts PDF pages. Selection is not a claim that the Item contains the full Work when alternatives or annexes exist. It stops at 403/429, transport failure, or below 15 GB disk space. `eu_stage.py` loads all enumerated Works and date evidence and then verified Item versions/pages into `eu_stage.duckdb`; repeat it after more downloads to update extraction status. This is the EU-only staging target. Do not write to the US/AU 09 database while its supervisor runs. The final merged view must preserve the EU Work namespace and source-specific denominator.

```bash
.venv/bin/python work_packages/M1_source_access/10_eu_cellar_acquisition/eu_acquire.py enumerate --start 1988-01 --end 2009-12
.venv/bin/python work_packages/M1_source_access/10_eu_cellar_acquisition/eu_acquire.py enumerate --start 2010-01 --end 2026-09

.venv/bin/python work_packages/M1_source_access/10_eu_cellar_acquisition/eu_stage.py --start 1988-01 --end 2026-09
```

After the 1988 Item download and stage have stopped, `eu_item_supervisor.py --start 1989-01 --end 2026-09 --delay 1.0` can resume the remaining months. It uses an exclusive lock, fetches one completed WEMI month at a time, stages its verified bytes, and records `manifests/item_supervisor_state.json` and a log. For a month with one non-access-limit failed stream it makes at most two additional attempts after 30 and 60 seconds; already verified files are rechecked without request pacing. It stops when a month has no WEMI checkpoint yet, when the public source blocks ordinary access, or at the 15 GB free-space floor. Restart it after an access/storage condition is resolved. Never run it concurrently with another `eu_items.py` downloader or `eu_stage.py` writer.

`eu_wemi_supervisor.py --start 2007-04 --end 2026-09 --delay 3.0` owns a separate exclusive lock for the official Work/English Item relationship harvest. It reads a saved 429/503 `Retry-After` checkpoint, waits until the stated time, then resumes that same batch. It stops on 403, transport errors without a retry gate, storage below 15 GB, or a retry limit. Its state and log are in `manifests/`. Never run it concurrently with another `eu_wemi.py` process. The 2007-04 batch 2 response was HTTP 503 with `Retry-After: 1800` and a not-before time of 2026-09-26 16:23:47 UTC.

`eu_transport_resume.py --delay 2 --cooldown-seconds 600 --max-restarts 5` may monitor one already-running Item supervisor. If that supervisor stops with a selected Item's checkpointed HTTP-0 `ProxyError`, timeout, connection error or SSL transport error, the wrapper waits ten minutes, checks that the state and exclusive Item lock have not changed, then restarts from that month. It caps repeats on the same Item and overall restarts. Source HTTP 403/429, unclassified failures, stage errors and the 15 GB storage stop never trigger an automatic restart. `manifests/transport_resume.jsonl` records each decision. Run at most one wrapper; it does not replace the Item supervisor's lock or source checkpoints. This bounded monitor was added after two transient local-proxy interruptions in 2003-05; its `--dry-run` correctly recognised the live supervisor without starting another.

The official 2011-11 batch 3 later returned a 122-byte HTTP 502 Bad Gateway page, which was checkpointed as a failed response; the next ordinary retry reconciled that month. The 2011-12 first batch then timed out after 120 seconds. The supervisor permits at most three ordinary retries for a 502 after 60, 120 and 240 seconds, or for a `ReadTimeout` after 120, 240 and 480 seconds. It does not treat error bodies as relationship CSVs. Any other un-gated status or continued failure remains a recorded stop.

`audit_work_enumeration.py` independently reads all saved Work-query CSV bytes, verifies their request hashes and byte counts, compares every row and source-page locator with the monthly manifests, checks date windows and official counts, and writes `reports/work_enumeration_audit.json`. The 26 September 2026 audit found 465 saved query pages, 50,578 manifest rows and globally distinct Works, and zero discrepancies. This verifies the metadata denominator only. `eu_report.py` separately reports how many English Item relationships and downloaded bodies have been established per month.

`audit_wemi_relationships.py` checks the exact requested Work partition recorded with every completed WEMI batch, its raw CSV hash and bytes, and every relationship row against the month manifest. It reads each batch's recorded Work list because the first 12 months used 10-Work batches before later 50-Work batches. The completed 465-month audit at 2026-09-26 23:56 UTC checked 1,359 saved batch pages, 47,472 Item-linked Work-months, 3,106 Works without an English digital Item link, and 211,690 relationship rows with zero discrepancies.

`eu_disposition_ledger.py` refreshes one row per globally unique Work in `reports/eu_work_dispositions.csv` and a compact count summary. It separates no English digital Item link, unrequested Items, verified downloads with extracted source text, scanned Items awaiting OCR, OCR candidates awaiting layout review, and known source placeholders. Its extraction status is an operational outcome, not a claim that the text is complete, relevant, or usable. Run it after a download or OCR cohort without opening the stage database.

When the selected Item is HTML or XHTML, `eu_items.py` saves the verified original bytes and extracts ordered source blocks into `.blocks.jsonl`, with a reproducible block ordinal/tag locator and a separate conservative whitespace-cleaned field. OLE Word `.doc` Items are converted by macOS `textutil`; the complete derivative text and its SHA-256 are saved, while `.blocks.jsonl` holds bounded text sections with explicit converter-based locators. A real 607,232-byte public DOC diagnostic produced 147 blocks of at most 3,992 characters; the temporary diagnostic copy was removed. `eu_stage.py` inserts HTML/DOC blocks into `eu_source_blocks`; PDF pages remain in `eu_source_pages`. `eu_report.py` reports both segment types separately. A real 1991-09 HTML Item has eight source blocks and a complete raw-byte hash, but its original contains a `>TABLE>` placeholder, so extracted text alone must not be taken as proof of a complete body. Other formats remain saved with an explicit extraction status until a suitable extractor is verified. At the 05:08 UTC disposition snapshot, 18 HTML Works carried this source-placeholder status and each had only one English Item in the frozen WEMI relationships. `eu_html_placeholder_audit.py` checked all 18 saved original HTML files: each contains literal `&gt;TABLE&gt;` source text and zero `<table>` elements, with zero audit issues. These Works remain review cases, not complete usable bodies.

`audit_item_bytes.py` verifies downloaded Item bytes and extraction derivatives without locking the active EU stage. Scan-only PDFs retain their original bytes and empty-text outcome. `eu_ocr_scans.py --binary /private/tmp/ft_eu_ocr_vision` can create separate Apple Vision OCR page/coordinate sidecars from those scans after compiling `eu_ocr_vision.swift`. It verifies every raw hash and page count, retains line coordinates, and labels results `ocr_candidate_text_extracted`; OCR errors and reading-order approximations require source-layout review before quotations or final usable-body claims. `eu_stage.py` verifies sidecar hashes and can load their page texts on a later single-writer restage. Do not run that restage concurrently with the Item supervisor.
`eu_ocr_backfill.py` obtains the Item-supervisor lock, verifies that the independent byte audit has zero issues, and idempotently restages only months with OCR candidates from the Work disposition ledger. On 27 September it restaged 28 months and loaded 69 OCR candidate versions into the EU stage after the Item downloader stopped on a single transport timeout; OCR remains unreviewed.
The 1997-10 Item timeout occurred within seconds of four US Federal Register SSL EOF failures. Both source supervisors stopped, preserved their logs, and the first ordinary retries after short cooldowns returned HTTP 200. Future failed request checkpoints are appended to per-object `.attempts.jsonl` before the latest checkpoint is replaced; the earlier transient failures remain evidenced in supervisor logs.

One 1994-04 PDF/A Item exceeded the bounded 100 MB Item cap. `manifests/item_selection_overrides.json` records its linked ordinary PDF manifestation of the same English Expression and the reason for that choice; the oversized PDF/A failure checkpoint remains. For an approved external Item store, link `raw/items_external` to the external directory only while the Item supervisor is stopped, then restart it. `path_for` keeps existing local files and checkpoints in place and sends only new Items to that linked store; stage metadata uses repository-relative symlink paths. Both the local stage disk and linked storage disk retain a 15 GB free-space floor, preserving working room for stage/OCR files and the next transaction.
