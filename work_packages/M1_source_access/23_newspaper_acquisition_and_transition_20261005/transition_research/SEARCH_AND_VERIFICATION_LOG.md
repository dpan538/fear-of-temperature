# Search and Verification Log

Research date: 5 October 2026. This was a bounded targeted review, not a systematic review or an exhaustive audit. All authored material and table field names are English. Work was confined to this `transition_research` directory; no subagents, extra chats, cross-thread messages, Git changes, shared logs, proposal edits or corpus writes were used.

The project direction, latest project-log entry, proposal source-method sections and separated newspaper/social-coverage decision were read before selecting research. The `nature-academic-search` skill was used for multi-source discovery and citation verification. Academic connector tools were unavailable in the session, so public publisher pages, survey producers, Crossref/DataCite metadata and university repositories supplied evidence. An OpenAlex request returned 429 and was not retried. No credentials or account sessions were requested.

Searches used variants of: social media overtakes print newspapers news source; newspaper social-media news use crossover year; Reuters Digital News Report longitudinal sources of news; newspaper displacement complementarity mobile news; incidental exposure social news; newspaper post-print attention; UK Ofcom news consumption; Digital News Report Australia; NZ On Air Where Are the Audiences; traditional social media agenda flow. Country-oriented searches covered the US, UK, Australia, New Zealand and named European countries rather than treating Europe as one population.

Crossref discovery included displacement/complementarity, incidental exposure, newspaper transition and traditional/social agenda dynamics. Exact DOI lookups verified the five journal entries in `references.bib`. DataCite exact lookups verified the Reuters 2015, Cornia 2018 and Australia 2025 report DOIs. Metadata was checked against original bylines/landing pages; Crossref HTML entities were normalized, journal issue years were separated from online-first years, and Neuman/Jang surname segmentation was corrected using the original publisher byline. Australia's DataCite record abbreviates the author list; the university's longer list is retained with the final author's observed initial.

Discovery leads not promoted to verified findings include an Australia2022 PDF exceeding the20MiB per-object bound, inaccessible COVID-era Netherlands articles, and inaccessible alternate full-text routes. Search snippets, aggregators and social discussion were not used to establish numerical turning points. AUT New Zealand trust results were recognized as a different measure and were not converted into news-use crossover evidence. Platform founding dates and newspaper circulation trends were not used to invent a crossover.

`scripts/fetch_public_evidence.py` records requested/final URLs, UTC retrieval times, content types, byte counts, SHA256 hashes and failures in `sources/RETRIEVAL_RECEIPTS.jsonl`. Requests are bounded to40seconds and20MiB per object; raw public source objects are retained unchanged. PDF text extraction and two visual page renders are derived inspection aids. Exact chart payload CSV files are derived from retained HTML. Network escalation was used only for public evidence retrieval after sandbox DNS failure. No private evaluator code, credentials, formal corpus database or government seal material was opened.

`scripts/build_package.py` serializes manually verified claims and source metadata into the four CSVs and BibTeX file. `scripts/validate_package.py` checks these outputs once, matches retained-source receipts to bytes/hashes, checks chart transcriptions and scholarly metadata, and writes `VALIDATION.json` plus `PACKAGE_MANIFEST.json`. Validation covers this research package only. It does not audit the corpus, validate survey truth, resolve pending access or establish population comparability.
