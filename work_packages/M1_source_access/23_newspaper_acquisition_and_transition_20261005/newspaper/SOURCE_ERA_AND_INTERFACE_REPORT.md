## Provenance and readiness interpretation

Directness refers to the recorded utterance. A publisher's own news article is direct media-discourse evidence, including when it quotes someone else; the quoted work/speaker still needs attribution. A library reproduction can verify the original publication while remaining an archival access route. A catalogue or newspaper report about an archive is indirect holding evidence. A mixed issue or site can contain original, syndicated, sponsored and archival works. Trinity News's candidate identity and several record-level mappings remain unresolved. No opaque confidence score replaces these dimensions, and provenance does not verify every reported claim.

No stable API or real newspaper adapter passed the complete applicable-era → index → independent article → full visible prose → retained raw → staging chain. Six compact native policy responses and tool-visible discovery observations are preparation evidence. Rights stops, authentication, tool failures, storage stops and unassessed periods are separately recorded. Remaining native routes and unused request allowances are retained for a bounded continuation; independent discovery is not declared exhausted.
