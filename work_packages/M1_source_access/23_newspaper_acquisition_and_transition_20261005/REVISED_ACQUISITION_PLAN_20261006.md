Preserve page/column/continuation coordinates, masthead dates, OCR uncertainty and issue-to-article relations. Native scanned issues remain evidence even when segmentation is pending. Do not wait for every issue to be perfectly OCR-clean before making progress on other permitted routes.
4. Save eligible bounded raw evidence using observed lawful public routes, then perform identity/date/body checks on the changed tranche. Keep failures and uncertain extraction states rather than discarding them to improve counts. Successful carriers proceed independently; no universal adapter, topic, fear, entropy, length or equal-count gate is added.
5. Freeze the tranche frontier/plan and exact resource preflight, execute, stage once and reconcile the changed tranche. Use the existing shared mutex, one local staging writer and cached receipts. Reopen a stopped exact target only with new specific access/transfer evidence and a bounded successor disposition; no automatic failed-target refill.
