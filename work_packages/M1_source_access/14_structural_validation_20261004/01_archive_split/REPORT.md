# Independent UK parliamentary reply-split validation

**Task 1 result, 2026-10-04.** The registered UK parliamentary written-answer and written-statement parent links were checked read-only against their saved source containers. The publication frame remains **1988-01-01 through 2026-09-21**. No topic, affect, risk or fear criterion was applied. No corpus record, raw file, formal database, frozen report or proposal was changed.

## Input, run and unit

The input is the 06 government-content DuckDB checkpoint (3,590,598,656 bytes; file mtime 2026-09-22 13:37:31 UTC) and the saved source paths linked from its content versions. The checked parent is a source reply item; question and reply paragraphs/blocks are child spans. A saved response's retrieval or content-update time does not establish that its body existed in that form on the publication date. The full pass read the registered UK parents once and parsed **27,124 of 27,127 linked saved containers**. Three Hansard list JSON containers lack the detail `Overview` structure needed for this adapter. The pass took 170.38 seconds; the shared heavy-I/O lock was held 162.326 seconds. The subsequent exception-tranche checks, including 943 initially flagged containers, nine date-heading ZIPs, one punctuated date ZIP, 321 Historic item pages, 135 flagged item-page continuations and an exact-ID omission lookup, are separately recorded in `INPUT_MANIFEST.json`. They did not rerun the full corpus.

The validator uses independent format parsers and compares source parent IDs or unique exact text signatures, publication dates, child locator roles and content versions, reply-block completeness, normalized span text, speaker where addressable, and order within each role. `RULES.md` defines every status and its limit. Ten focused fixtures pass, including omitted reply blocks, crossed parent boundaries, duplicate ownership, multipart replies, grouped questions and date headings. An additional bounded check aligned one real saved parent from each of the Historic ZIP, Hansard detail, modern list, ParlParse and archive HTML formats before the full pass. The 1991 Historic item HTML tranche was checked separately after its format was identified in the output.

- **Historic XML:** 124 date conflicts are confined to two 2003 range-heading clusters. Stored dates 2003-07-10 and 2003-06-01 are outside the corresponding saved heading ranges (7–13 October 2003 and 20 December 2002–6 January 2003). A range heading does not itself identify the exact day of each reply, so preserve that uncertainty rather than replacing each date with a range endpoint. Seven further parents cross question/reply roles and two omit addressable reply nodes. Five remain insufficient: four unkeyed text signatures are not unique/exact, and one speaker comparison differs.
- **Historic keyed source groups:** 202 groups in five saved ZIPs lack a parent linked to that ZIP. Exact source-ID reconciliation found none registered elsewhere under the same Historic source ID. They are **candidate** omissions in approved department sections, concentrated in 1992 (17), 1994 (45), 1997 (84) and 2000 (56). Check the earlier exclusion and overlap ledgers before proposing any insertion; a missing same-source ID alone does not prove eligibility or historical version identity.

Modern `groupedQuestions` links with identical saved answer text support **6,044 parent rows in 2,405 explicit answer groups**. Another 6,287 linked clusters (16,441 parents) have differing or missing saved answer text and are recorded as `different_text_or_incomplete` in `answer_groups.csv`; this does not fail each parent's own split. An empty or different answer-group comparison is not a source or fear-measure exclusion gate.

## Provenance and next handoff

Historic bulk XML, official Historic item pages, Hansard detail/archive pages and modern parliamentary API lists are saved official text routes for their respective recorded utterances. ParlParse is a mirror: its **mirror-internal** split alignment is checked here, while official source equivalence remains a separate provenance question. Original article/utterance presence and structural mapping do not establish the truth of the claim or any affective interpretation. Earlier cross-source title/date mirror candidates remain candidate identities, not automatic duplicates. Retrieval, publication, source content update and snapshot times must stay distinct.

The coordinator can use `exceptions.csv` to scope existing-data repairs, `omission_reconciliation.csv` to review candidate missing Historic groups, and `answer_groups.csv` to preserve legitimate shared modern answers. No repair, backfill, acquisition or downstream Task 3/4 launch was performed here. Climate relevance and emotion/fear interpretation remain later-stage work under the fixed interval and source frame.

For a **fresh** validation with the final parser, run `.venv/bin/python validate_archive_splits.py run --root <repo> --output <new-empty-directory>`. The correction scripts and preserved `*.full_pass` / `*.before_*` outputs document how this single execution's initially flagged tranches were resolved; they are not prerequisites for a fresh run with the current validator.
