# Corpus-wide provenance and date-logic validation

**Run:** 4 October 2026 UTC. **Publication interval:** 1988-01-01 through 2026-09-21 inclusive. September 2026 is partial. This read-only validator checked saved structural metadata and known evidence ledgers. It did not change raw data, formal databases, source filters, the proposal or frozen reports, and did not retrieve new records or label climate, affect or fear.

## Execution and counting boundary

The [CLI](validate_provenance_logic.py) applied the [ten named rules](RULES.md) to each saved unit in 11 source-specific frames. [Input checkpoints and filters](INPUT_MANIFEST.json), [frame inventory](frame_inventory.csv), [110 rule-coverage rows](execution_coverage.csv), [record-level annotations](findings.csv), and [source-quality summary](source_quality_summary.csv) make the run reproducible. The three DuckDB files were opened read-only while holding the common `fcntl` lock. The [run log](RUN_LOG.json) records acquisition at 06:21:15 UTC, release at 06:21:20 UTC, and completion at 06:21:43 UTC. The wait for another worker's heavy I/O was 112 seconds. Lightweight postscan reconciliation added the already-read auxiliary evidence paths and their modification times to annotation keys; it changed no outcome or source record.

## Outcomes and named exceptions

Every frame-rule row has an eligible count partitioned into `supported`, `conflict`, `needs_review`, `uncheckable`, `not_applicable` and `unassessed`; all 110 rows have `unassessed=0`. No rule reported a demonstrated structural `conflict` under the fields it could compare. The 57,759 emitted annotation rows comprise 1,488 `needs_review` and 56,271 `uncheckable` results. These are **rule instances**, often several for one parent, not unique documents or a corpus error rate. Other uncheckable rule outcomes that would only repeat absence without a bounded record action remain in coverage rather than being materialised as individual findings.

- **Date role and cutoff.** The 119 GOV.UK source timestamps whose source-local calendar day differs from UTC are `DATE_CROSS needs_review` (113 DEFRA, six historical). Three cross a month boundary in the prior date audit. The stored local day is not automatically wrong; choose and log a single analysis-day convention before refreshing those three monthly bins. The 65,988 Questions/Statements API parents were compared to saved `dateAnswered` rather than the potentially earlier `dateForAnswer`. Eight AU candidates have verified original dates; two other September 2026 month-only candidates overlap the 21 September cutoff and require an original issue day. Another 811 AU candidates have unknown original dates. CMS creation and retrieval times never substitute for publication. No proven post-cutoff original date was detected among the comparable fields.
- **Identity and issuer.** Four US parents have repeated Federal Register document numbers in two distinct dated pairs: `95-24211` on 29 September and 13 November 1995, and `C1-2014-16556` on 28 and 29 July 2014. Canonical URLs and issue dates distinguish the parents; a possible correction/republication relationship needs evidence before any version link or merge. One AU candidate is a CSIRO-authored report hosted by DCCEEW; the original author/commissioning role remains a `needs_review` attribution, not a claim-truth judgment.
- **Version, status and attachment.** All 1,020 DEFRA parents carry `blocked_pending_ethics_route` while linked saved text versions exist; 34 of 35 historical GOV.UK parents carry `downloaded_pending_extraction` with linked text. The validator marked those 1,054 status relations `needs_review`, preserving the text and labels until their semantics are reconciled. It did not treat multiple objects, attachments or shared archive containers as extra parents. EU has 100 OCR/layout candidates, 23 HTML table placeholders, one failed selected Item and 29 later saved zero-text versions whose earlier disposition still says `selected_item_not_requested`: 153 Works need bounded content/status review. The 31,464 EU Works without a checkable saved version and 14,954 US parents without one are explicit `CONTENT_LINK uncheckable` annotations, not failed-source or semantic exclusion decisions.
- **Original route versus body.** The route ledger classifies 67,043 UK parents as original-utterance route candidates and 180,992 as archive or mirror reproductions; it classifies US issue reproductions, 47,472 EU Item-route candidates and direct Guardian article/petitioner expressions separately. For 3,106 EU Works no English digital Item link is recorded in the prior route export. Eight AU landing-to-original pairs were previously checked; 813 original alignments remain unresolved. A reachable URL or saved hash does not establish original issuer, exact historical wording, or substantive truth. The four Guardian body checks concern current observed pages; the remaining 392 URLs have metadata but no documented body check.

The [quality summary](source_quality_summary.csv) keeps route-derived directness, date/content metadata outcomes and bounded individual review counts separate. Its prior review bases are heterogeneous: eight purposive UK parents, 125 EPA bridge parents, eight AU original pairs, four Guardian body checks and 21 petition reads; there is no corpus-wide individual-original review for EU. These counts do not estimate population reliability. No indirect or mixed route was identified within these *selected* frames; that does not imply zero such sources in the wider project. An original news article is direct evidence of that outlet's discourse and a petition is direct evidence of its author's expression, including when either quotes others. Government risk statements and media quotations require separate holder attribution in later interpretation.

## Handoff and limits

The [record annotations](findings.csv) give a source-specific unit, rule/version, observed value, expected relation, outcome, evidence path/locator, snapshot key, check time and bounded action. [Schema notes](ANNOTATION_SCHEMA.md) propose a derived review table; no formal schema was altered. [Tests](test_provenance_logic.py) cover cutoff edges, date roles, local/UTC conventions, shared FR numbers, stale status, hosted originals, attachment multiplicity and legitimate future-mentioned events. The independent [output verifier](verify_outputs.py) checked coverage arithmetic, all frame inventories, deterministic IDs, evidence links, four Guardian checks, the 119 UK date cases and the two `95-24211` parents; both checks passed.

This is structural support only. It does not verify every original body, the historical wording of later-modified pages, every parliamentary answer boundary, or the truth of source claims. The five reconnaissance-only routes in [frame inventory](frame_inventory.csv) have unknown denominators, not zero observed records. Earlier pooled government source-text presence at 465/465 study months is a separate recorded snapshot and was not rerun here. Source/genre/period concentration may justify a future independent frame with a defined unit, interval and stopping rule, after coordinator review of Tasks 1 and 2. No repair or acquisition was started.
