## What changed, and what remains

Newspaper repaired the named directory/body-state paths and loaded genuine
surplus into all 116 affected Green Left months: **338 new complete IDs**.
However, 69 of those months now contain exactly four articles and 29 contain
exactly five. No replacement count-stop predicate was found in the changed
scheduler. A concrete state-bridge defect remains: the dispatcher suppresses
generic Green Left body jobs, while newly discovered issue candidates are
not consistently merged into its live ready overlay. Fresh eligible surplus
can therefore remain queued without a body opportunity. This needs an
in-process queue-to-ready merge and an eligible-body fallback, plus actual
Load/replay evidence. These shallow plateaus and unfinished inventories
still require source-era continuation. A successful code regression is
distinct from exhausting an eligible archive.

The remaining newspaper empty months are **1988-11, 1988-12, 1989-04 and
1990-11**. This interval added no qualified 1988–1990 article. Named routes
retain unresolved original-article boundaries/date conflicts and real
permission, paywall, challenge and PDF-slot limits. Restricted routes were
not bypassed. Twenty later empty months were recovered, principally through
conventional/local native archives. Ten titles first contributed qualified
bodies, including Berkeley Daily Planet, Enfield Dispatch, Waltham Forest
Echo, Cambridge News and Te Awamutu News. Falls Church News-Press and
Limerick Post together still supply 44.41% of this round's newspaper additions.
Twenty-four title IDs remain below the 50-title development objective; that
objective is not a collection stopping condition.

Social added 14 actual community source frames: nine Discourse communities
and five Stack Exchange communities. These new frames contribute 27,652
dated bodies. A structural grouping identifies **23 named forum/Q&A community
families**: 16 discussion-forum families and seven Q&A communities, after
collapsing two Minneapolis archive interfaces and excluding platform
instances and mailing-list namespaces. The strict forum count therefore
remains below the more-than-20 development objective. This is not 23 verified
independent operators, 23 general-public populations or complete historical
coverage. Community purpose and technical/specialist frames remain visible.
Python mailing-list bodies did not grow this round, but their 613,429 retained
dated bodies still make up **62.14%** of the social pool. No corpus-wide topic,
emotion, automation or noise labelling was performed.

The recent-period 2026 imbalance persists. Keeping only the baseline
contributing source frame produces **57.07%** for 2026 in the matched
January–August 2016–2026 comparison. Neither comparison controls each
source's recoverable inventory or selection probability. No attribution to
a real climate event or population expression rate follows. Original
records and legitimate peaks remain preserved.

Publisher, platform, software, instance and community parent relations are
separate dimensions. The parent figure reports bounded mapping coverage
and unresolved mass, rather than inventing an independent-parent total.
Current ownership evidence cannot automatically validate historical owners.
Parent diagnostics never gate grabbing or terminate production.

## Stops and capacity

Newspaper's watchdog stopped requests at the original **04:27:04 AEST**
deadline. Social stopped source requests at **02:21:12.904 AEST**, about
two hours six minutes early, because its remaining permitted complete
operations failed the separate 15 GB cumulative guard. Its last Load was
02:20:00 AEST; terminal exports completed afterward. This was a resource
stop, not an object-count or coverage-completion claim. Physical disk and
the shared 30 GB envelope were not exhausted.

After final exports, social retained 14,852,484,938 bytes and had
147,515,062 nominal bytes left, before prospective operations. This package
copies 9,455,144 bytes of social deliverables, which must also be counted
prospectively even though they reside outside the live social directory.
Conservative remaining social capacity is therefore **138,059,918 bytes**
before new control/output growth. The unchanged minimum collector reserve
model requires 117,506,048 bytes before raw/body/row costs, leaving a narrow
20,553,870-byte margin. Small whole-native-unit operations may fit, subject
to live checks that include raw, parser, database, journal, metadata and
delta exports. No sustained four-hour yield, byte increase or weakened
physical safeguard is implied. A genuine no-fit result must be reported
honestly. Large per-entity provenance/attachment exports remain in the
original local delivery; they are referenced by final manifests rather
than duplicated in this publication.
