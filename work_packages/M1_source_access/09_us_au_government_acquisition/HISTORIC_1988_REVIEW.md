# 1988 Federal Register Index route: current evidence and boundary

The official [GovInfo annual Index](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GPO-FR-INDEX-1988/pdf/GPO-FR-INDEX-1988.pdf) was saved on 26 September 2026 as a 127,788,013-byte PDF with SHA-256 `07a918da5fc83b8a12477d888ed2b1c56875ae8efc7bf61b28409416588ace36`. Its 152 PDF pages contain an extractable OCR text layer. PDF page 32 was rendered and visually checked against extracted text: three printed columns, a complete `Environmental Protection Agency` heading near the end, followed by `RULES` and the first printed-page references.

The bounded parent-heading pass identified `Energy Department` on PDF pages 30–32 and `Environmental Protection Agency` on pages 32–40. It yielded 35 DOE and 828 EPA numeric *candidate locator tokens* in their `RULES` and `PROPOSED RULES` sections (753 distinct EPA page numbers). These are **not document counts**: a printed page may be referenced more than once, OCR may misread numbers, a correction may be separately indexed, and one entry may have multiple page references. The DOE heading explicitly points to subagency sections including FERC, Bonneville and power administrations; these have not yet been reconciled to the modern API hierarchy.

GovInfo's official link service resolved all 780 distinct 1988 candidate printed pages to issue dates and PDF jump fragments (863 index rows). The fragments require scan-level verification, and a redirect alone does not prove the start/end of an independent rule Work. The `53 FR 21646` locator led to the saved and hashed 9 June 1988 issue PDF. Its page 36 contains preceding FCC documents and the beginning of a DOE **final rule**, “Acquisition Regulation”; the DOE rule continues through page 39 and ends at FR Doc. 88-13009, before a Commerce rule. The saved 4 January 1988 issue contains an EPA **final rule**, “Pesticide Tolerances for Myclobutanil”, beginning at `53 FR 20` / PDF page 28 and ending at FR Doc. 87-29874 on PDF page 29. The saved 5 January issue contains the EPA Illinois hazardous-waste rule beginning at `53 FR 126` / PDF page 26 and ending at FR Doc. 88-50 on PDF page 27. `historic_sample_extract.py` preserved source excerpts with PDF page locators and cleaned versions, excluding neighboring articles. These are three independently bounded historical rule samples, not a denominator.

Issue review also exposed a false-positive index token: `53 FR 3` came from a chemical name in the EPA index text, while issue page 3 belongs to another agency. The raw locator-token totals therefore cannot be treated as document counts or even a clean set of genuine citations; each candidate requires issue-level validation.

The separate official 1989–1993 annual indexes are also saved and hash checked. Parent DOE/EPA RULES and PROPOSED RULES sections produced 893, 685, 630, 677 and 1,096 candidate locator tokens respectively. Across all six years, all 4,844 locator rows / 4,385 distinct printed pages now resolve through the official link service to 1,325 distinct issue dates, with zero unresolved links. These are still noisy page locators, not document counts. Linked subagency sections and issue-level boundaries still need reconciliation before any year can be reported as a full historical parent count. The historical series remains separate from the 33,544-record 1994+ API series.

The 1989 `54 FR 17734` DOE locator was independently checked after the official 25 April 1989 issue PDF was acquired as 67 contiguous verified HTTP byte ranges. The completed 70,106,331-byte PDF has SHA-256 `4eb761495fe36d5e3d3419f77ada0e9fe53345d551341da80dfca5a3667d6e27`. PDF pages 58–62 contain DOE's final rule “Acquisition Regulations; Government Travel Discounts to Cost Reimbursement”, beginning after FCC material and ending at FR Doc. 89-9909 before a NOAA rule. The same issue's pages 93–94 contain EPA's proposed rule “Approval and Promulgation of Implementation Plans; Indiana”, beginning after DEA material and ending at FR Doc. 89-9872 before an FCC proposal. The annual index labels match the independently observed `RULE` and `PRORULE` actions for these two locators. Source and cleaned excerpts are saved with page locators and individual hashes. These are the fourth and fifth bounded samples, not a 1989 document denominator.

Three more January 1988 issue PDFs (6, 7 and 8 January) were fully acquired as verified byte ranges under a 30 GB local free-space floor. There are now seven saved official issue PDFs across 1988–1989 and six hash-verified annual indexes. The 6 January issue exposed a GovInfo link-fragment misalignment: its `53 FR 392` link says PDF page 168, but page 168 is Reader Aids; the actual printed page 392 is PDF page 160. The latter contains EPA's final rule “Requirements for Preparation, Adoption, and Submittal of Implementation Plans”, bounded through PDF page 164 and FR Doc. 88-177. It is saved as the sixth independently bounded rule sample. An independent header audit of all 34 candidate locator rows in these seven issues found 25 exact fragment/header matches, two corrected fragments (the other is `53 FR 347`: PDF page 115 rather than 123), and seven rows whose printed-page header was unreadable in OCR. The CSV does not infer a page when the header cannot verify it. These are only page alignments; `53 FR 347`, for example, points to another agency's material and is not an EPA rule. A link-service issue date is useful source routing evidence, but its PDF fragment is **not** accepted as a verified page locator until checked against the printed page in the scan. Other annual-index page tokens remain candidates, not articles.

Rendered issue pages 58, 62, 93 and 94 were visually checked against the OCR extraction: DOE begins in the third column of page 58 and ends in the first column of page 62; EPA begins in the second column of page 93 and ends in the second column of page 94. The clipping markers exclude the preceding and following articles on those shared pages.

The seven remaining `printed_page_not_found_in_header` rows in the automated page-alignment audit were rendered and visually checked on 27 September. All seven official PDF jump fragments point to the visibly printed page number. Five pages contain EPA rule material: 53 FR 241, 243, 470 and 21641, plus 54 FR 17707. The other two, 53 FR 3 and 5, show Federal Crop Insurance Corporation rules, so their EPA index tokens are agency false positives. The [visual supplement](raw/us_fr_annual_index/saved_issue_visual_alignment_supplement.csv) records the issue PDF hash, page and observed agency content separately from the automated OCR status. A visual page match still does not establish a whole article boundary; mixed pages such as 53 FR 243 and 54 FR 17707 contain successive rules.

GovInfo's [Federal Register help](https://www.govinfo.gov/help/fr) says the digitized pre-1994 volumes are downloadable at the full-issue level, whereas later volumes have article-level files. A deterministic three-date-per-year sample of 18 official `HEAD` responses is recorded in `issue_size_sample.csv`: every response was HTTP 200 `application/pdf`, with sizes from 27,223,223 to 630,543,879 bytes and a median of 105,839,795 bytes. Multiplying that median by the 1,325 candidate issue dates gives a **rough** 140,237,728,375-byte planning figure; it is not an exact storage requirement or an article count. Local free space was about 35 GiB at that planning check and about 20 GiB on 27 September after more EU originals were saved, insufficient for the whole historical issue queue alongside the active US and EU collections. The historical originals remain limited to the already hash-verified issues and six bounded rule samples until a suitable storage target is available.

On 27 September, a separately published [Federal Register archive slice](https://archives.federalregister.gov/issue_slice/1988/2/5/3369-3382.pdf) returned HTTP 200 `application/pdf` and `Content-Length: 3827670` to a bounded `HEAD` request. This is a possible smaller original for *known* page ranges. A guessed 6 January 1988 `241-243.pdf` key returned S3 HTTP 403, so arbitrary ranges cannot be assumed to exist. The archive slice route does not itself enumerate EPA/DOE rules, resolve index false positives or identify article boundaries; it does not replace the official issue-level historical audit.
