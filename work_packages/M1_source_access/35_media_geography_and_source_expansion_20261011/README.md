## Concrete expansion routes

The [social route matrix](SOCIAL_ROUTE_MATRIX.csv) distinguishes documented public reads from credentials, retention and environment limitations. [Bluesky actor feeds](https://docs.bsky.app/docs/api/app-bsky-feed-get-author-feed), [Mastodon account statuses](https://docs.joinmastodon.org/methods/accounts/#statuses) and [WordPress.com public blog posts](https://developer.wordpress.com/docs/api/1.1/get/sites/$site/posts/) provide concrete nonforum interfaces. Next acquisition should expand ordinary-account/author/site cohorts and dated historical tails, retaining institutional/editorial roles separately. Public availability is not an open-content license or proof of full historical coverage.

[Blogger](https://developers.google.com/blogger/docs/3.0/using) and Flickr require application identifiers/keys. [X full-archive search](https://docs.x.com/x-api/posts/search/introduction) requires paid/developer access. [Meta Content Library](https://www.icpsr.umich.edu/sites/somar/meta-content-library) requires approved research access and environment-specific retention/export. [YouTube's policies](https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/developer-policies) require refresh/deletion of non-authorized API data within 30 days, which is unresolved for this project's permanent frozen raw model. No account, key purchase or application is made. Tumblr and PeerTube remain conditional where official documentation or per-instance permission was not verified.

The [newspaper candidate table](NEWSPAPER_ROUTE_CANDIDATES.csv) contains 28 leads across UK, Ireland, Denmark, Portugal, Malta, NZ, AU and US. Six title identities are primary-verified; other leads remain explicitly provisional or access-blocked. None has acquired articles in this supplement. Candidate names do not increase the accepted 30-title count. Related Social Spider titles require separate title identity and shared-publisher mapping. Admit eligible historical inventories and full bodies directly into the regular ELT pipeline once source-specific access/retention checks pass; paywall previews, post-cutoff-only launches and exhausted PDF allowances do not become production routes.

Newspaper expansion also has actual unfinished native inventory: 12,260 untouched candidates in the 116 named legacy rows, plus existing conventional-title frontiers. Candidates are not guaranteed complete articles. Prioritize real source-era opportunities and UK/European/NZ breadth alongside deeper existing archives. Do not replace the removed two-work rule with per-country or per-month quotas.

The [2×/3×/5× quantity scenarios](MULTIPLE_SCALE_SCENARIOS.csv) express the new quantity requirement without promising a time-bound yield. A 3× comparison requires another 61,408 regular newspaper articles and 1,977,402 dated social bodies. Increasing technical-list volume alone would not resolve the missing nonforum source frames. Quantity scenarios and the existing 50-title development need are reporting objectives, never production stop conditions.
