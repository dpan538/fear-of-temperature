# Source provenance and targeted supplementation audit

**Checked 4 October 2026 UTC.** Study publication interval: **1988-01-01 through 2026-09-21**, inclusive; September 2026 is partial. This is a read-only source-quality assessment. Retrieval, saved-body modification, ledger export and report times remain separate from publication dates. The earlier 465/465 pooled government source-text-month result is a dated coverage snapshot, not a quality or topic count and was not recomputed here.

## What was counted

The [source-quality summary](source_quality_summary.csv) has one row per source route/category; its route classifications are **rules applied to metadata**, not 1:1 verified originals. The [ledger](source_quality_ledger.csv) contains those 14 rule rows and 30 individually reviewed parent/Work rows. These denominators are **different source frames and nonsynchronous checkpoints**. Do not sum them into a corpus total or interpret the pilot source frames as all media/public discourse. The EU disposition export is earlier than the atlas's later EU checkpoint, and its 12,347 extracted-text disposition count must not be mixed with the atlas's 15,299 readable-Work count. The US 08:34 export similarly must not have 125 later bridge parents added without parent-level reconciliation. No inferred directness class is a verdict on factual truth. There are no rule-identified indirect/secondary news or public sources in these *specific* frames; this does not establish a zero count for the wider project. A journalist's article is direct evidence of that outlet's discourse even when it reports someone else. A petition is direct evidence of its submitter's expression, including a rejected submission, while its publication status remains a separate variable.

The UK route split is 65,988 official Questions/Statements API plus 1,020 DEFRA and 35 historical GOV.UK parents as original-utterance route candidates, and 136,082 official Historic Hansard, 36,462 official Hansard/publications archive, and 8,448 ParlParse/TWFY mirror parents as archival reproductions. An official archive can preserve a primary record; the mirror route needs explicit upstream alignment before claiming exact official-version equivalence. EU Work–Expression–Manifestation–Item links describe a reproduction route, but 35,001 selected Items were not requested at this checkpoint. The AU eight original-pair checks include a CSIRO-authored report hosted on a DCCEEW page; hosting does not make its original speaker DCCEEW. The [fixed source contracts](../../09_us_au_government_acquisition/FILTER_CONTRACT.md) and [EU contract](../../10_eu_cellar_acquisition/FILTER_CONTRACT.md) define those source frames.

## Individual verification and exceptions

Within the **30 reviewed** parents/Works, 22 had individually supported identity mapping, 23 had supported date mapping at the recorded precision, and 18 had supported content mapping to the saved body/primary file or reviewed authorial fields. Twelve cases retain at least one partial or pending identity/date/content check. These numerators use explicit statuses in the sample CSV, not extrapolation. For every route, `denominator minus sampled_n` in the summary is **individually unassessed** by this audit. Version status is separate: a checked current article, GOV.UK page, archive JSON or saved byte hash does not establish that its text equals the text at first publication.

Five sampled rows carry a provenance/structure flag, with different meanings:

- **Two GOV.UK status/mapping discrepancies:** `doc_835f5a934eabaad87e68` is marked `blocked_pending_ethics_route`, and `doc_b893248b8a640a7a8e7d` is marked `downloaded_pending_extraction`, yet both have linked saved page/PDF versions with text segments. The two status fields need reconciliation; the publication parents and original bytes remain intact.
- **Two distinct dated Federal Register parents sharing `95-24211`:** 29 September and 13 November 1995 have separate canonical URLs, issue dates and pages, each matched to saved GPO text and byte hash. The possible republication relation is a follow-up, not authority to merge on number alone.
- **One issuer-scope ambiguity:** an August 2005 primary PDF is attributed in prior browser evidence to CSIRO Marine Research but is hosted by DCCEEW. It is direct to CSIRO's report; its classification as the DCCEEW government's own utterance needs a documented commissioning/role decision.

Other bounded findings: the selected Historic Hansard parent `doc_335778ff5eb2c04a2829` uses a synthetic derived ID absent verbatim from the original bulk XML; title and question text match, while exact answer-span alignment remains partial. The two UK API records match saved official list IDs and `dateAnswered`; `dateForAnswer` is earlier for one 2026 parent, so those date fields cannot be interchanged. Four selected CELLAR Item byte hashes match the disposition ledger, but the OCR candidate and HTML table placeholder lack validated full text. One EU Work has no English digital Item link. An AU protocol page has a primary file plus four appendices but no evidenced original issue date; another AU plan is labelled only September 2026, which cannot settle eligibility at the 21 September cutoff. Three of four sampled December Guardian URLs have checked canonical publication metadata but no inspected body; the previously checked December body was modified years after initial publication. For the three petition examples, opened time and created time are kept distinct; the rejected example has no opened time.

`substantive_claim_dispute` is **not assessed in all 30 checks**. These are provenance/attribution/identity issues, not findings that the records' substantive claims are true or false. The presence of a claim disagreement in a source would not itself make the source record invalid.

## Targeted supplementation and stopping

The [supplementation queue](supplementation_queue.csv) names 11 bounded actions with exact targets, original routes, uncertainty, priority and stopping rules. Immediate decisions concern the September 2026 AU plan's cutoff day, the two GOV.UK status discrepancies and the two dated `95-24211` parents. The rest are conditional parent-level issuer, original-date, primary-file, mirror-alignment or item-completeness checks. No global AU catalogue, EU no-Item, US pre-1994 print archive or 17,238-item US pending-body sweep follows from these findings. Missing access is recorded as missing access, not zero source output.

For a future derived review table, add `source_frame_id`, `parent_id`, `object_or_version_id`, `evidence_role`, `source_issuer`, `original_url`, `archive_url`, `directness`, `classification_basis`, `publication_date_precision`, `retrieved_at`, `content_version_time`, `checked_at`, separate identity/date/version/content-mapping statuses, `access_status`, `provenance_conflict_type`, `substantive_claim_dispute`, `evidence_reference`, `review_scope` and `follow_up`. This is a **review-table proposal**, not a live database migration. Preserve raw records and existing source filters.

## Boundary for the next stage

This assessment concerns dated source presence, readable saved text and source provenance only. It did not label climate relevance, risk, affect or fear, and it did not use any such measure to include, exclude or rank a source. Those analyses require later source-aware retrieval and validation. The current review also did not retest the previously recorded 465/465 government source-text-month result. The 30-case sample, source-specific export times and unresolved body-version histories limit the strength of any whole-corpus authenticity claim.
