## Prioritized findings

1. **2026 concentration is chiefly a collection-frame issue in the social baseline, not a demonstrated bad-record rate.** Four Mastodon instances contribute 87,566 dated bodies, all within recently recovered 2026 ranges: Aus.Social 21,786, mastodon.ie 21,878, mastodon.me.uk 22,083 and SFBA.social 21,819. Their previous last-successful batches still returned 40 native statuses dated respectively in July, June, April and July 2026. Three cursors remained active; the Irish route recorded a timeout near the deadline. This supports backward recent-first local-timeline traversal as a contributor. It does not establish a source launch date, exhausted archive, complete cache history, or genuine historical growth in expression. Official [timeline documentation](https://docs.joinmastodon.org/methods/timelines/) defines `local` and the `max_id` bound; named saved-batch diagnostics are pinned in `s4_mastodon_diagnostic.json`. Original HTTP Link headers were not retained in that predecessor diagnostic, limiting cursor-continuity verification.
2. **Historical recovery changes the pooled share without validating or invalidating the existing 2026 cohort.** The later social snapshot adds 263,652 usable dated bodies: 253,852 before 2026 and 9,800 in 2026. The entire 2026 increment comes from three continuing Mastodon instances: Aus.Social +3,263, mastodon.me.uk +3,314 and SFBA.social +3,223. The twelve 2026-contributing social sources are unchanged. The large older Python-list tranche is a separate technical mailing-list frame; it lowers the pooled year share without establishing balanced public coverage. Native/core/dated-body additions have different totals and must not be substituted for each other. See `2026_SOCIAL_SNAPSHOT_COMPARISON.csv`.
3. **The newspaper 2026 slice has two source frames and an evidenced repeat-content candidate set.** Flagstaff contributes 171 articles and Northern Rivers Times 666. Flagstaff is NZ; Northern Rivers Times is AU, following the preserved package29 erratum. All 837 metadata IDs and URLs are unique, with no repeated version IDs or known work-family keys in this slice. However, 27 exact-body-hash pairs involve 54 Northern Rivers Times IDs: 26 pairs span different publication days and nine span different months. Their 810 distinct recorded hashes are a sensitivity count, not a verified count of independent works. Same content can be republished legitimately, while duplicate observations or a wrong boundary require different treatment. Twelve purposeful native JSON checks cover source endpoints, July endpoints and three named hash pairs. All twelve saved native IDs/dates/links match the export; the three pairs share normalized visible native text. Two pairs differ in rendered HTML; the July pair has identical rendered content. These are bounded automated structural/hash checks, not truth or historical-body-equivalence verification. Exact results are in `worker/NAMED_NEWSPAPER_RECORD_CHECKS.json`. A supported work-link candidate preserves both publication IDs/dates and never confirms an entity alias merely from a hash.
4. **The July Northern Rivers Times flag is bounded and not event attribution.** It has 196 article IDs, compared with a same-source available-neighbor mean of 59.4 (ratio 3.30). Only five of six adjacent months are available because October lies beyond the fixed endpoint; September is partial. July retains 181 distinct recorded body hashes. Repeat-content candidates therefore deserve lineage checks, but neither a hash sensitivity count nor the peak ratio justifies deleting the month. The baseline source flags also identify August Aus.Social (12,000) and SFBA.social (11,616), where unrecovered earlier months and partial future context strongly affect the reference mean. Earth Science's August ratio flag concerns just six bodies against 1.25; it is a low-count diagnostic, not an event claim. No natural event or climate cause was independently attributed.
5. **Date-field agreement is narrower than historical verification.** All 837 newspaper export days agree with their reported publisher timestamp day; the field names identify native public JSON dates in site time. Native creation and modified fields are kept separate. Some Flagstaff modified times precede scheduled publication dates, which alone does not establish a defect. The social sample checks 24 earliest/latest 2026 native entity metadata endpoints, not 24 qualified bodies: 17 lie inside the study day interval and seven are October context/native entities. Their presence in a native catalog does not prove inclusion in dated-body counts; record-to-qualified-body membership was not re-audited. [Mastodon Status documentation](https://docs.joinmastodon.org/entities/Status/) separates the local database ID, federation URI, creation time and edit time. The official [Bluesky post lexicon](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bluesky-social/atproto/main/lexicons/app/bsky/feed/post.json) describes `createdAt` as client-declared. Thus native repetition of a timestamp is not independent historical publication proof. Nine inherited non-2026 era conflicts remain named (six Bluesky, three Earth Science), with no forced date or 2026 count correction.

## Parent provenance and historical validity

All **14 sources contributing dated text in 2026** were researched: two newspapers and twelve social sources. `PARENT_SOURCE_EVIDENCE.csv` contains 98 dimension-specific rows, separating acquisition family, operator organization, publisher organization, platform network, software, instance and community. Confirmed means the stated bounded relation is supported; it does not mean every record/date/content claim is correct. Valid-time starts and ends remain unknown unless separately evidenced; current ownership is not projected across historical years.

- Flagstaff's [publisher contact page](https://devonportflagstaff.co.nz/contact-us/) names Devonport Publishing Ltd and an Auckland office. Northern Rivers Times' [contact page](https://thenorthernriverstimes.com.au/contact-us/) supports NSW offices and Heartland Media contact association, while its current network brand is separate from proven legal publisher identity. An email domain is insufficient ownership evidence; the web result for that contact domain was non-confirmatory and is preserved as an access/evidence limit. Keep `northern_rivers_regional_media` as the recorded acquisition family pending a dated publisher mapping.
- [Mastodaoine CLG's own page](https://mastodonie.github.io/mastodaoine-clg.html), linked from saved mastodon.ie metadata, establishes current operator duties. The other three Mastodon instances retain separate operators: UK saved metadata describes volunteer administration and names a hosting provider; SFBA describes a volunteer team and its [community hub](https://hub.sfba.social/); Aus.Social metadata describes its own admin/mod structure. These facts do not make the Mastodon software organization their publisher. The Aus.Social description's “thundertoot” wording is preserved alongside the same domain/title, not treated as a new source or an invalid identity.
- Lemmy NZ and Aussie Zone saved native site metadata identify distinct instances, Lemmy versions and enabled federation. Their site-record timestamps are not proof of the first original utterance or all community history. Legal operator organizations and exhaustive child-community membership remain unresolved. Two saved documents lack an inherited expected payload hash; this review hashes the original saved files/payloads without claiming an earlier independently verified binding.
- Python's [terms](https://discuss.python.org/tos) name the Python Software Foundation as operator. OSM's [Foundation terms](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Terms_of_Use) identify its forum service, while the forum footer identifies Discourse software. Straight Dope's [terms](https://boards.straightdope.com/tos) name Sun-Times Media, LLC, but an undated/current terms assertion does not prove operational ownership continuity. These three forums sharing Discourse do not become one publisher or community.
- Earth Science and Sustainable Living are distinct communities on the same Stack Exchange network. [Current network terms](https://stackoverflow.com/legal/terms-of-service/public) name Stack Exchange, Inc.; site tours support community identities. The underlying implementation/version is not independently established here. [Bluesky's terms](https://bsky.social/about/support/tos) distinguish Bluesky Social, PBC, the application and the wider AT Protocol. Its bounded acquired actor/feed frame is not the entire network or ordinary public.

Directness is relative to the utterance: original newspaper publication is direct evidence of media discourse, including reporting and quotations; a native authored post is direct evidence of its author's expression. An operator is not automatically the speaker. Federation/context copies, archival reproduction, syndicated/quoted origins and unresolved mappings require record-level relations. This review does not rank public/secondary sources below official sources, assign one quality score, verify claim truth, infer author nationality, or establish population representativeness. Public reachability, local retention, licensing and redistribution remain separate inherited facts; this review grants no new content-use permission.
