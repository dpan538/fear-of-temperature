# Complete independent articles as the newspaper coverage baseline

**Quantity threshold superseded:** Dai subsequently made two articles the hard minimum, with a unique ID and source link for every article. Apply the [current two-article rule](2026-10-06-two-article-minimum-and-stable-identifiers.md). The earlier one-required/two-target interpretation below is preserved as history; the complete-article and no-chunking rules remain in force.

Date: 6 October 2026. Authority: Dai clarifies that newspaper coverage must use independent articles, at least one to two per time point, and rejects continued text splitting as the acquisition unit.

Apply this to the existing **465-month** calendar, 1988-01-01 through 2026-09-21. A month meets the minimum newspaper baseline only with **at least one complete, independently published article**; the target is **two distinct articles**. Keep geographic/source-month ledgers separately so pooled presence never certifies an empty region. A pending or empty cell remains a gap, not zero expression. If a different time resolution is later adopted, evaluate it from article publication dates rather than treating chunks as new observations.

The acquisition/counting unit is the publisher's original article with title or equivalent native identity, source/edition, supported publication date, a traceable complete body and one article identifier. Retain author/byline when supplied; an unsigned editorial is not excluded merely for lacking a personal byline. A webpage is a transport container, not automatically an article. Indexes, tables alone, captions, advertisements, whole issues, incomplete previews, missing liveblog components and arbitrary text passages cannot satisfy this baseline.

Do not split newspaper text into fixed-length/model passages during acquisition or inflate counts using PDF pages, columns, continuation pieces or OCR segments. A single original article spanning columns/pages may be reconstructed into **one complete article body**, preserving its page/column/continuation evidence. Geometric extraction restores an existing article; it does not create several articles. Do not concatenate unrelated original items to manufacture one article. Generated segment markers do not belong in the restored article text; keep coordinates and technical details in sidecar metadata.

Distinct articles means distinct original publication items, with known duplicate, mirror, version and same-work relationships accounted for. Two URLs, body versions or reposts of the same article do not satisfy the two-article target. This does not require proving a journalist's global originality or treating all syndicated journalism as ineligible: preserve the published article and record its underlying work relationship, counting a known same-work duplicate once for the minimum distinct-article target.

Package24's frozen result of 45 readable HTML units and three mapped article parents remains a transport/derived-text snapshot, not a new claim of 48 verified complete independent articles. Its 86 readable-unit months require an additional article-based reconciliation. A named new US 2008-04 candidate is a table-only page and cannot certify article coverage. The three mapped PDF parents have native titles and bylines, but their completeness/OCR reconstruction needs article-level evidence. Preserve their original bytes and receipts rather than silently rewriting the frozen report.

The authorised follow-through is one bounded local task in the same acquisition owner: assess the new tranche, selectively establish one or two article candidates in earlier observed months using existing metadata/checkpoints, record complete/pending/non-article/duplicate states and publish a new 0/1/2-or-more article ledger. Do not recrawl, scan or re-clean the entire corpus. Only named derived defects and selected candidate bodies are examined. No new network requests, formal import, budget enlargement, social collection or hidden evaluator access is released by this clarification.

This is a structural unit/completeness requirement. No topic, fear, emotion, uniformity or minimum-length test is added. The source calendar and raw evidence remain fixed; complete article coverage is distinct from archive completeness, national representativeness and later research measurement.
