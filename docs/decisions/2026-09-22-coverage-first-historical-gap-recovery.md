# Decision: prioritise historical coverage over corpus growth

Date: 2026-09-22
Status: approved by Dai in conversation

## User direction

“早期文件依旧需要替代方案，coverage是一个优先级很高的事项，然后才是distribution，混合不清可以直接标记为放弃，不需要滥竽充数，实际上我们的现代数据已经足够了，只是要把数据缺口补足。”

## Decision

1. Historical coverage recovery is the first priority. Early policy documents require an alternative-source investigation alongside the known parliamentary date gaps; they must not be deferred simply because modern material is abundant.
2. Stop expanding the modern corpus. Access existing modern records only where necessary for identity matching or a source-overlap check. Do not use modern volume to compensate for historical absence.
3. Keep policy publications, written answers and written statements separate. Recovering an answer does not fill a missing policy-publication slot.
4. Abandon inclusion of records with inseparable mixed text or unreliable speaker/department attribution. Preserve raw evidence, identity and an explicit exclusion reason; do not fabricate attribution, repeatedly repair them, or count them as recovered usable records. This is exclusion from the usable corpus, not deletion of source evidence.
5. A transport failure alone is not an ambiguity exclusion. A genuine unavailable document remains a coverage target for alternative retrieval. Likewise, a container is not itself a usable statement; retain independently identifiable children only.
6. Update coverage evidence as targets are resolved. Defer distribution tables/figures until the historical recovery tranche is complete or its remaining external barriers are documented. No repeated distribution-only work packages.
7. No numerical quota or minimum count substitutes for coverage. Use enumerated source/date/department/genre targets and state denominator uncertainty. Never interpret an inaccessible archive or missing index as zero historical material.

## Consequences

- Priority tracks: early policy-publication alternatives; 2004-Q4 Commons seam; missing 2010–2014 answers. Evaluate routes for these gaps without adding countries, broadening department/topic scope or restarting modern acquisition.
- Named mixed cases Asda and Self-regulating Bodies should be dispositioned as excluded from usable records on the documented ambiguity, retaining their source evidence. Companies House remains an access-recovery target unless separate evidence establishes ambiguity. The unresolved statement container must not be inserted as a statement.
- Reuse the existing database and stable identities. A mirror copy is a representation of an existing source, not automatically a new independent document.
- Keep all changes in project decision/log/acquisition records; do not update the proposal.

This decision supersedes earlier suggestions to defer early policy-source alternatives behind distribution or modern-corpus work. It does not claim any recovery has already occurred.

## Additional approved reporting requirement: Figure 5

Dai additionally requests that future corpus summaries include a fifth figure showing monthly/quarterly coverage from the study start to the collection endpoint.

- Figure 5 is a full-period chronological coverage chart, not another record-count distribution. Use 1988-01-01 to the actual collection cutoff for the current study; never advance the endpoint automatically to the report date.
- Show policy publications, ministerial written answers and ministerial written statements separately, with source/department subrows where needed to avoid hiding partial coverage.
- Provide a monthly evidence CSV and an overview readable at normal report size. A quarterly overview with monthly detail in HTML or a zoomed panel is acceptable. Include empty/unknown periods and visibly mark partial first/last bins.
- Distinguish bounded-source targets recovered, partial recovery, incomplete/unknown enumeration, access blocked, not requested, unsupported source periods and confirmed zero; show verified non-sitting/not-applicable periods separately when relevant. Use accessible colours plus patterns/labels. No unknown-to-zero conversion.
- Display a numerical coverage percentage only where an enumerated and appropriately labelled denominator exists. Known-target processing, usable-text recovery and historical population coverage are different concepts. Excluded ambiguous items remain visible in accounting; exclusion alone cannot turn a bin into complete usable coverage.
- Quarterly aggregation must retain any unresolved component and unknown denominator; never average away a missing month or count a single covered month as a covered quarter. Source overlap must be deduplicated and source scopes must be explicit.
- Derive the chart from the same versioned coverage table as the report. Reuse existing coverage plotting/data code; Figure 5 is a concise full-period synthesis rather than a separate verification project. Retain the established first four figures, with no need to redesign them.
