## Spoken scope transition

Following my supervisor's feedback, I am now focusing the study on Australia. I will use texts from Australian institutions, news media and public communities. This gives the project a more manageable scope, so I can spend more time checking the evidence and doing the analysis. I will compare how warming is discussed, what changes around relevant events, and how people describe possible harm. Community location and an individual author's location remain separate.

The wider idea behind Fear of Temperature remains important: people learn to understand and respond to rising temperature through shared records and experiences. Australia is the case through which I will study that idea. My aim is a clear framework for attribution, analysis and comparison, supported by original passages.
