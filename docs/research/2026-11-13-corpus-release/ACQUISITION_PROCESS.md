# Acquisition Process

## Scope and record units

The Australian empirical frame covers 2003–2025. Australian institutional, publisher, community or explicit textual context supports source association; it does not prove each author's residence. Personal blogs, diaries, authored articles, publications, forum posts and public comments retain their native identities and genres. Metadata, search hits, candidate URLs, acquired fields, document versions and formally admitted records are different units.

The broad frozen v3.15 matrix preserves standalone parent terms and all 638 children. Its 24 sub-parents cover somatic experience, worry, fear, ecological grief, metaphor, cultural learning, anger and backlash. Mild, medical, indirect, neutral, positive and mixed expressions remain eligible. A query hit is not a human-validated affect label; broad expressions require contextual interpretation downstream.

## Discovery and weighted search

Five semantic discovery families receive soft effort weights: somatic/daily experience 1.60, psychological/cultural response 1.60, ecology/memory 1.50, irony/metaphor 1.35 and broad exploration 1.00. Individual saved jobs retain their actual family and weight. Parent defaults do not overwrite job attribution.

Supplementary year effort uses the saved existing-corpus counts, with `minimum(year counts) / count(year)`. This favours historically sparse years without forcing equal new counts. Final corpus counts must not be substituted into that frozen scheduling snapshot. Year, city and genre terms are optional search variants; native dates control eligibility. Eligible 2019 narratives remain in scope.

The search evolved from explicit year/city/genre combinations to single-child searches, original parent terms, genre-free alternatives, geographic OR alternatives and nonphrase variants. Broad and deficit streams were interleaved, with the final saved configuration assigning four broad opportunities per deficit opportunity. These are attempted search opportunities, not record quotas or inclusion probabilities.

The [offline query builder](../../../scripts/search/build_query_jobs.py) adapts the production query-building function to explicit frozen inputs. It writes designed jobs only and cannot dispatch searches or access the corpus. [The saved registers](../../methodology/search/README.md) preserve 942 actual registrations separately from 4,169 saved jobs; not every designed child was individually searched.

## Native discovery and complete text

Web search supplied candidates, while source-advertised public API collections, unfiltered metadata continuation, native author/year archives, feeds, pagination and linked publication pages supplied additional discovery. Metadata enumeration does not earn body or formal-record credit. Request batches, returned fields and corpus records are reported separately.

The later throughput changes restored cold-source metadata service, ready-body priority and fallback lanes; reduced repeated queue decoding; compiled unchanged source-access rules; and cached receipt metadata while continuing to verify sealed input bytes. These repaired scheduling and bookkeeping bottlenecks. They did not waive access, identity, date, originality or complete-native-field checks. An isolated decoder speedup or overlapping rolling-hour count is not evidence of a sustained collection rate.

Original native prose is preferred, especially approximately 1,000–2,500-token authored narratives. There is no universal hard length threshold. A complete shorter post, written introduction or licensed excerpt remains its actual native genre; it is not promoted to a full article, book or audiovisual transcript. Extended thread or quotation context does not increase owned-text length or record counts. Search-result snippets are never represented as acquired native prose.

## Access, identity and boundaries

Use documented or publicly advertised routes and preserve source-specific usage/access frontiers. A denied, empty, protected or malformed response is a frontier, not evidence of historical absence or permission to bypass access restrictions. Large forums remain eligible subject to those same conditions; diversity is a discovery preference rather than blanket forum exclusion.

Native source-qualified IDs, original/canonical URLs and work/version/alias relations support deduplication. Exact body hashes are evidence for relationship review, not permission to erase independent speakers or historical occurrences. A later body correction or context addition creates a traceable version and does not earn another record. A shared headline alone does not prove duplicate authorship.

The production review distinguished main prose from interface recommendations/navigation, external republications, quoted passages and uncertain original-work attribution. Confirmed or unresolved failures entered reversible holding tracks. Holding records and source evidence were retained; the 1,383 current-phase holds are not all proven copies. Native publication, CMS modification, rendered updates, capture and narrated event time remain separately recorded.

## Durable writing and closure

Candidate processing, native-body capture and sole-writer formal admission had separate stages. Inputs and receipts bound native identity, body/raw SHA and batch provenance. Only actually committed, deduplicated, eligible records earned increment credit. Formal union accounting reconciled baseline plus increment across preserved SQLite and DuckDB stores; physical rows and metadata observations did not substitute for that union.

The final phase completed 26,383 gross new receipts, retained 1,383 reversible holds and reconciled 25,000 actual net additions. In-flight complete batches drained naturally. Final native replay, generic chronology qualifications, preservation and distribution/requirement evidence preceded completion. The [closeout](README.md) states the exact verification scope and historical evidence limits.

No acquisition, body replay, database mutation, raw snapshot, migration or model run is performed by publishing this documentation. Detailed runtime queues, agent dispatch, locks and per-target receipts remain local. The public [manifest](PUBLICATION_MANIFEST.json) records which compact sources support the released projections.
