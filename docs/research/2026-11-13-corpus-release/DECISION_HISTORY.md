## Continuing methodological commitments

Preserve original text, positive/neutral/mild/mixed cases, native voice/date lineage and recurrent expressions. Do not remove later positive material or source peaks to manufacture a learning curve. Query weights describe selection effort; source composition, changing retrieval, archival availability and year-specific measurement error must be examined as alternative explanations.

The government activity inventory motivates the observation range and provides context. It does not make every official publication a physical event, a verified event onset, an exogenous treatment or an institutional position. Future event identity and pre/during/post windows require independent evidence and explicit handling of overlap.

The [manifest](PUBLICATION_MANIFEST.json) identifies the supporting original sources. This compact publication is not a byte-exact archive of all historic request queues, controls or chat handoffs.
