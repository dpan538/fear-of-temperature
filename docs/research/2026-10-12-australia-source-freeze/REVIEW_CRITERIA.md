# Review criteria for an Australian source-scope freeze

12 October 2026. Read-only review of accepted source-frame and source-month metadata; no raw-body audit, acquisition, corpus freeze, semantic labelling or model run was performed. This recommendation follows Dai's latest emphasis: **diversity, coverage and distribution**, with **100 GB as the main total Australian working-data budget**. Earlier 50 GB preferred / 100 GB alternative notes remain historical planning evidence; a current plan should state the amended priority explicitly.

## Freeze evidence, not a diversity score

Prepare a versioned source manifest with source/native IDs, Australian-context evidence, genre, publisher/community identity, access and retention conditions, observed/applicable eras, original-date precision, version relationships and unresolved issues. Separate retained candidate stock, usable analysis inputs, conditional supplements and international methods references. Keep complete source records available irrespective of neutral wording or absent fear cues.

Review a **source × year/month × native-form** matrix before declaring analysis windows. Its cells should retain distinct complete works, readable original bodies, segments/paragraphs, versions, and missing/uncertain dates. Add token/length distributions only when measured on the declared view. Distinguish non-existent/pre-foundation periods, observed records, applicable-but-unobserved periods, access failures and unknown applicability. A missing row is not proof of zero discourse. Do not place year-only originals into invented months.

Describe diversity through evidenced source types, locality, publication form and contributor structure. Report source shares by year and matched period, plus parent shares where mappings are verified. Keep legal publisher, acquisition/API parent, independent publication work and author/account relationships separate. Show unresolved mapping counts/bounds. No composite score, fixed title/community minimum or government/news/public volume ratio is needed to freeze a useful question-specific frame.

Investigate peaks and plateaus against adjacent months, boundary incompleteness, duplicate URLs/IDs, syndication/mirroring, version multiplication, attachment splitting and date assignments. Use named evidence to resolve defects; retain valid peaks. Compare matched complete-month windows and stable source frames before interpreting changing shares. Independently dated event evidence is required before attributing a peak to an event.

## Units, comparisons and the budget

**Publicly available database** describes access; **public/community expression** describes the discourse frame. Millions of openly deposited parliamentary paragraphs must not inflate the public-expression count or government independent-work count. Preserve sitting → procedural unit/utterance → paragraph lineage, dated speaker/office evidence and quoted-holder distinctions. Party metadata alone does not establish executive responsibility. Similarly, thread replies, boosts, quoted remote bodies and repeated content require their native relations.

RQ1/RQ2 need actual overlapping, readable source/time evidence and disclosed source composition. Where government overlap remains unverified, begin a supported two-frame or within-frame comparison and report its scope; do not borrow international monthly coverage. RQ3 remains later. Climate relevance/similarity and affect/fear validation belong to subsequent analysis, not structural source-inclusion gates. Broad negativity and risk language are not fear by themselves.

The **100 GB peak ceiling** covers Australian source evidence, canonical text, lineage, passages, vectors/indexes, model/cache files, results, checkpoints/backups and maximum concurrent temporary writes. Protected international holdings stay preserved outside it; referenced Australian originals remain accounted for without requiring duplicate copies. Component reservations may move after measurement. The prior physical-space snapshot does not establish present 100 GB feasibility, and the existing 15 GiB floor remains additional.

Estimate from observed source/native-form lengths and passage multiplicity, not an arbitrary 100,000/one-million passage cap. Avoid unnecessary resegmentation, repeated full-text copies, multiple vector representations or an index without a demonstrated query need. Use resumable bounded input/output: the existing whole-matrix helper does not provide this. If the justified design exceeds capacity, document which representation, operation or comparison causes the excess and revise it scientifically; do not delete originals, equalise volumes or truncate context to make counts fit.

Freeze acceptance should record the chosen question/source/time matrix, named structural issues and their dispositions, unit/lineage contract, unresolved limitations, measured capacity assumptions and the permitted next analysis step. It is a reviewable boundary for analysis, not a claim of national representativeness, full-calendar completeness or a new collection/model release.
