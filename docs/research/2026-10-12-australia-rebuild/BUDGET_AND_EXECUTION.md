# Primary 100 GB envelope and staged execution

12 October 2026. The ceiling is **100,000,000,000 bytes of peak new Australian working data**, including source packages, text, lineage, passages, vectors/indexes, caches, results, checkpoints and concurrent temporary output. International holdings remain preserved outside this scientific envelope, but still occupy the same disk. The earlier 50 GB preference is historical. Neither ceiling is a fill target or evidence of physical capacity.

## What was measured

A read-only length check inspected **127 archived bodies** from nine Australian newspaper source IDs and two community contexts, preserving original stores. All sampled donor hashes matched when checked against the correct representation: newspaper derived text, social original native body/HTML. Sample selection and aggregate lengths are in [LENGTH_SAMPLE_SUMMARY.json](LENGTH_SAMPLE_SUMMARY.json). It samples undisposed archive candidates rather than certifying every frozen accepted ID; no acceptance count is revised. The sample is deliberately nonrandom: up to 12 date-spaced newspaper IDs per source and three eligible social bodies near the beginning of six named months. Short posts, long articles and source differences remain visible. No text copies were published.

Two bounded RAPID Parquet blocks additionally confirmed real text, session/speaker joins and **245,760 paragraph rows**, including empty texts and unresolved speakers. Their UTF-8 means are 396.65 and 306.74 bytes; file blocks mix dates and can cut across a sitting or procedural unit. They do not estimate a representative national length distribution or the exact recent-window paragraph count. See [government confirmation](GOVERNMENT_CONFIRMATION.md).

The calculation uses 220 **whitespace-word** windows with 20-word overlap as a planning proxy. These are not model tokens, an adopted passage policy or thesis segmentation. Transferring sample means to frozen candidate stock gives approximately 46.9 MB of text and 80,390 proxy windows for all old AU media/community candidates; the 27-full-month recent subset gives 17.0 MB and 40,654 windows. These conditional values are not measured whole-corpus sizes, confidence intervals, acquisition quotas or final analysis denominators. Actual tokenizer/source-aware passage counts must replace them before vectors.

## Reproducible sensitivity, with unmeasured overhead explicit

[The calculator](estimate_verified_budget.py) and [its result](VERIFIED_BUDGET_SENSITIVITY.json) separate observed counts/length checks from assumed metadata, overlap, index, cache and operation buffers. Dense vector payload is exactly `N × dimensions × bytes/value`. An optional index is modelled as one extra dense copy plus 128 bytes per item; this is a capacity sensitivity, not a measured index build. The illustrative streaming operation has a 100,000-row output shard, 256 MiB input buffer and 512 MiB journal/atomic-write allowance. This bounds a file operation and does not cap eligible corpus size. An index implementation requiring a full temporary copy must add that copy explicitly.

The full-parliament case assumes one vector per raw paragraph, including empty/procedural rows, and transfers the two nonrandom block means solely for stress arithmetic. It is not the selected analysis workload. Further splitting, title vectors, versions, backups, source rehydration or additional build copies can raise every value. The 1024-dimensional legacy matrix case already exceeds the primary envelope under these assumptions. Float16 would halve the dense payload, but is not adopted without retrieval validation; this arithmetic does not choose a model.

At the recorded snapshot, roughly **35.90 GB** was free. After the **15 GiB floor** and separate **48 MiB recovery allowance**, the upper additional operation headroom was **19.74 GB**. It is a changing upper bound before concurrent operations/reservations, not a release. Every full-parliament embedding case exceeds that local headroom even when below 100 GB. Disk fit does not prove RAM or throughput. Complete-operation preflight must include download parts, decoded input, database/journal growth, unfinished shard, index staging and any retained backup. Preserve one writer per new store and serialize heavy I/O; old leases remain expired.

## Concrete execution sequence

1. **Archive by reference and establish the new boundary.** Use the [inventory](ARCHIVE_INVENTORY.json), schema/extractor preservation map and [independent schema/adapter](REBUILD_ARCHITECTURE.md). Preserve old bodies/versions/counters in place. Create a versioned source register, rights assertions and archive-to-new mapping; keep named legacy structural holds. No physical archive copy or destructive clearing is needed for this step.
2. **Government expression inventory before a new collector or final window.** Pin RAPID DOI/version/checksums and session/speaker joins. First inspect already obtained bounded text for dated climate explanations/risk/warning candidates, with explicit inspection denominator, original locator and speaker/quoted-holder uncertainty. Validate relevance and attributed warnings before choosing a priority minimum analytical window. The existing recent subset is verification only; no final 27-month restriction is adopted. Keep full-period availability/inspection/absence states and both chambers/political/procedural distinctions. Confirm supplier/underlying terms and complete-operation footprint before any larger ready-data intake. Date order must not be inferred from row groups; inspect known session identity candidates against originals and official calendars. No full-parliament vector run follows automatically.
3. **Selective media/community carry-forward.** Enumerate the frozen accepted recent IDs, keeping articles, posts, replies and versions separate. Apply source/date/unit/content/rights checks and old named dispositions to explicit rows. Copy or reference only verified records into the independent view, with original and derived hashes and immutable lineage. The current 2,821 / 5,884 / 24,237 recent stock counts are opportunities, not already imported quantities. The length sample alone is not an intake manifest.
4. **Resolve only named gaps.** Obtain an executable recent conventional-media source/title and explicit original-text mining/export permission if national media comparison is retained. PM's verified single-ID route can recover a named executive original; its blocked date catalogue cannot define a complete denominator. Petition routes remain conditional until real inventory and original text are accessible. Compare an official sitting calendar before treating a parliamentary empty month as missing acquisition. Do not substitute signatures for civic authored texts, headlines for articles or modern platforms for historical public expression. Each small gap fill needs an explicit endpoint/source/time/native-unit manifest and peak-operation calculation; this plan creates no collector lease.
5. **Structural acceptance and evidence-supported analysis.** Maintain source × month × unit presence, applicability, missingness, genre/parent composition, corrections and quarantine ledger across the full 465 months. Keep access-induced concentration and valid peaks. The explicitly requested bounded government climate inventory is an analysis ledger, not a structural deletion/inclusion gate. Use its validated evidence plus comparable original availability to justify minimum priority windows, then validate similarity/passages and supported RQ1/RQ2 comparisons. Full-period pooled coverage and separate role gaps remain long-run macro-distribution requirements; do not force month-filling, equal volume or all three roles every month. RQ3 fear interpretation remains later. No prediction model or prototype is added.

Minimum-window selection retains contiguous comparable baseline/non-hit months. Freeze source/genre/unit/dated-role and before/after rules before comparing outcome magnitudes, with independent event dates for RQ2; inventory-derived window discovery is exploratory. Report inspected eligible readable original units and available/recovered inventory separately, deduplicate versions under explicit identity rules, and validate retrieval before using climate positives over that inspected denominator. Uninspected, missing-body or unresolved-role cells stay null; a lexical zero is not a validated climate absence or a government-role zero. No fixed month count substitutes for these requirements.

## Work and machine time

The ready-data path is provisionally **62–128 active researcher hours** to a first supported comparison: 6–12 for source/unit/rights acceptance, 8–16 for the importer/carry-forward, 12–28 for structural and dated speaker/office checks, 8–16 for AntConc/context reading, 8–16 for later retrieval/passage/model validation and 20–40 for RQ1/RQ2 interpretation. These are revised judgement estimates, not measured labour or a promise. Access waits, unknown supplements, full fear annotation, machine runtime and the prototype are separate. At eight active hours/day this is roughly 8–16 working days; no change to Dai's previously recorded stage dates follows.

No model benchmark ran. For the conditional 80,390 media windows, encoding alone would take 22.33 / 2.23 / 0.45 hours at assumed 1 / 10 / 50 windows per second. The full 6,991,659-window stress case would take 1,942.13 / 194.21 / 38.84 hours at those rates, before parsing, I/O, joins, index construction or evaluation. These sensitivities are not device performance claims. Benchmark actual tokenizer throughput, length tails, cache size, peak RSS and disk growth only after a model/data flow is selected, then use `N/r + measured non-encoding costs` and resumable shards.

**Executed:** small public interface/text checks, metadata-month compilation, archived-body length checks, arithmetic and offline adapter tests. **Not executed:** active database creation/cutover, physical archiving, large import, corpus-wide segmentation/embeddings/labels or thesis analysis.
