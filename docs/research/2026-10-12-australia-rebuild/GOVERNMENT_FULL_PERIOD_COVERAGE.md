Report government/media/public gaps separately; all three roles need not occur in every month. Do not force acquisition, positive topic hits or fear observations to fill a calendar.

**The study is not complete.** RAPID supplies a useful parliamentary core, PM Transcripts supplies an operational named-ID executive route, and official Hansard/Gazette holdings provide evidenced historical bridges. ## Current evidence and actual historical omissions

The checksum-matched RAPID sitting table records **3,300 rows over 249/465 pooled months**, leaving 216 calendar months with no exported row. That is dated export presence, not 249 completely imported/readable original months. Only two nonrandom text blocks have been checked. A no-row month can reflect recess, export omission, access failure or a date/unit issue; it is not automatically zero government expression. [Verified sitting inventory](government_session_month_counts.csv).

A concrete official-calendar check identifies historical omissions. The export has **House 2 / Senate 1 rows in 1996**, and **0/0 in 1997**. [APH's Senate sitting statistics](https://www.aph.gov.au/Parliamentary_Business/Statistics/~/link.aspx?_id=2A55B28674694255AF931AC210BBFCD6&_z=z) report **71 Senate sitting days in 1996 and 82 in 1997**. Thus 1997's absence is an export gap, not a year-long recess. Its 1998 Senate count is 59 against 57 official days, which also requires unit/date/version reconciliation. Official statistics group some cross-midnight sittings; annual differences cannot be promoted to precise missing-speech counts. Retain all original IDs/dates pending evidence-linked corrections.

For every chamber/year, compare the export against **actual dated sitting lists or journals**, not just planned calendars. A verified non-sitting month is inapplicable to a chamber-sitting unit while PM/ministerial/policy expression may still exist. The government source frame is broader than Parliament; opposition, independent and quoted speakers do not automatically become executive-government voices.

For missing PM originals, the official collection points to NLA's archived prime-minister websites beginning in the Howard era. This cannot supply pre-web 1988–1995 material. The [APH 2024–25 annual report](https://www.aph.gov.au/-/media/05_About_Parliament/54_Parliamentary_Depts/543_DPS/Publication/AR_24-25/DPS_Annual_Report_2024-25.pdf) documents older mixed press-release/speech/clipping information files. Such archival pages need issuer/date/original-work verification; a clipping is not government discourse just because Parliament holds it. No bulk export or provider message is authorized by those holdings statements.

## Coverage and minimum-window decision

The [bounded climate inventory](GOVERNMENT_CLIMATE_INVENTORY_PILOT.md) supplies traceable candidates and five contextual checks. It does **not** determine sufficient full-period density or a final minimum window. Maintain separate states for uninspected, unavailable, readable source presence, lexical candidate, context-reviewed climate discussion, validated zero within a declared frame, and source-specific non-applicability. None of the pilot's no-hit months is a validated absence of government climate discussion.

Freeze sources, genres, native units, dated roles and applicable calendars before estimating outcome magnitudes. Keep a contiguous baseline with its no-hit months and independently justified before/after rules; do not use the positive convenience-sample months as the analytical population. Require independent dated event anchors and explicit source/role missingness. When role or a fear measure is unsupported, that measure stays null while supported climate-topic and macro-association work continues. Lead/lag alone does not establish causal influence.

Preparation order is concrete: reconcile the official chamber calendar and the RAPID ID inventory; map known PM climate contexts with speaker attribution; resolve lawful PM enumeration; audit Gazette year/notice metadata only where it serves the declared source frame; then bind a bounded readable intake and structural acceptance. Full-period pooled coverage remains an objective to demonstrate with actual accepted monthly units, not a fixed acquisition quota or automatic positive-hit rule.

Public access, reuse permission and derivative distribution remain separate. The **RAPID deposit's** CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 terms are distinct from underlying Parliament text rights, whose copyright page was inaccessible in this check. Historic Gazette item notices require source-specific assessment; PM text has CC BY 4.0 with specified visual/item exceptions. Register automation requires respecting its crawl-delay and load conditions. Old holdings, schemas, counters, raw data and expired leases remain untouched.
