## Original units and the full-calendar contract

For every month, maintain separate fields for known readable original units, applicable source existence/holdings, unobserved or inaccessible text, pending dates/rights, and verified absence within a defined inventory. The pooled indicator is the union of eligible dated originals, with native units and roles retained; it is not a sum of incompatible articles, posts, petitions, pages or parliamentary paragraphs. Multiple role memberships for the same original must not create additional pooled originals.

A newspaper letter can supply evidence of an author's mediated public expression while the newspaper container is evidence of editorial publication. Preserve separate author/holder, letter boundary, container/page, publication date, editing/abridgment and quotation relations. An expert, elected official or organisational submission/letter is not automatically ordinary public expression. A host in Australia establishes the declared source context, not every writer's historical residence.

The actionable order is: **join the government dated readable inventory; identify actual explanatory/warning windows; selectively carry existing Australian material; then verify a named media or civic bridge where it matters.** Track every unresolved full-calendar month and each role’s independent gaps even when a government original fills pooled presence. Full-period structural distribution remains visible during bounded analytical validation. No sample, nominal holding era, title count or licence statement alone completes the research-period claim; no opaque score, equal-volume quota or forced semantic selection replaces it.
