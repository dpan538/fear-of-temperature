# Australian official text routes: evidence and feasibility

Research snapshot: 12 October 2026 (Australia/Brisbane). Read-only source research; no full dataset, transcript corpus, model or collection process was downloaded or run. The publication interval remains **1988-01-01–2026-09-21**, with September partial. A deposit version, retrieval date or later archive capture is not a publication date or evidence of historical-body equality.

The sealed Australian government baseline remains seven verified original publication parents, five with day/month precision and two year-only, inside an overlapping 821-entry catalogue candidate frame. These routes are prospective options for a named Australian comparison; neither catalogue candidates nor international pooled 465-month presence establishes Australian coverage. See the [scope decision](../../decisions/2026-10-12-australia-analysis-scope.md).

## Practical source routes

| Route | Evidenced era and text/access | Rights and principal workload |
|---|---|---|
| **Department of the Prime Minister and Cabinet: PM Transcripts** | Approximately 26,000 speeches, releases and interviews since the early 1940s, across the whole collection rather than the study interval. [Official description](https://pmtranscripts.pmc.gov.au/). Pre-1997 material has OCR text plus original scans; late-1997 onward is principally HTML from the PM website. [Format evidence](https://pmtranscripts.pmc.gov.au/about-collection). A [documented developer interface](https://pmtranscripts.pmc.gov.au/developers) supplies an XML URL index and full-content XML by transcript ID. | Transcript text is [CC BY 4.0](https://pmtranscripts.pmc.gov.au/copyright), excluding specified visual material. Low extraction complexity for born-digital text; substantially greater date/OCR/speaker QA for older documents. Exact eligible count, current XML return quality, historical completeness and request throughput remain unmeasured. **First choice for executive discourse.** |
| **Federal Parliament: House/Senate Hansard through ParlInfo** | Official searchable proceedings include 1988: [dated Senate example](https://parlinfo.aph.gov.au/parlInfo/search/display/display.w3p%3Bdb%3DCHAMBER%3Bid%3Dchamber%2Fhansards%2F1988-12-21%2F0079%3Bquery%3DId%3A%22chamber%2Fhansards%2F1988-12-21%2F0000%22). Parliament reports online holdings from 1981 before its 1901–1980 digitisation. [Official history](https://www.aph.gov.au/About_Parliament/Parliamentary_Departments/Department_of_Parliamentary_Services/Publications/Annual_Reports/Annual_Report_2010-11/Part_3). HTML fragments, complete PDFs and XML/SGML support original-unit reconstruction; accessible downloadable derivatives are described below. | Parliament's [default terms](https://www.aph.gov.au/Help/Disclaimer_Privacy_Copyright) are CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, with exceptions and third-party rights. Public access is not an unrestricted transformed-corpus redistribution licence. Main APH pages were robots-blocked to this browsing tool; indexed official text and ParlInfo examples were available. No live bulk API throughput/completeness test was performed. **Best legislative comparison route, with dated role mapping.** |
| **Office of Parliamentary Counsel: Federal Register of Legislation** | Full laws and related documents, principally HTML with PDF/DOC/ePub downloads. [Official holdings](https://www.legislation.gov.au/help-and-resources/using-the-legislation-register/about-the-federal-register-of-legislation): Acts as made from 1901; most legislative instruments/explanatory statements from 1991; complete point-in-time compilation versions from 2013, with earlier holdings partial. The [free, keyless REST API](https://www.legislation.gov.au/help-and-resources/using-the-legislation-register/data-share-and-reuse) returns JSON and documents. | [Default CC BY 4.0](https://www.legislation.gov.au/terms-of-use), subject to item exceptions. Automated access is documented; respect crawl delay and source-load conditions. Useful for bounded policy/explanatory-statement context. Version multiplication, commencement/registration/publication distinctions and repeated consolidated text are the main QA work. Statutory wording alone is a poor substitute for expressive executive discourse. |
| **Treasury: Budget archive** | The [archive](https://archive.budget.gov.au/) includes historical annual material well before 1988 and through 2025–26; current material is separately on Budget.gov.au. The [1988–89 page](https://archive.budget.gov.au/1988-89/index.htm) offers one 13.3 MB financial-relations PDF, not evidence of a complete budget series. The [1996–97 page](https://archive.budget.gov.au/1996-97/index.htm) includes speech, statements, releases and MYEFO as PDF/Word. | [Current default CC BY 4.0](https://budget.gov.au/legal-notices.htm), excluding specified content. Older documents may state different terms, as in the [1996 Charter statement](https://archive.budget.gov.au/1996-97/ministerial_statements/honest.pdf); retain item-level rights. Annual/event-based policy comparator rather than a guaranteed monthly series. Medium effort for document boundaries, tables, appendices and older PDFs. No bulk API was verified. |
| **National Library: Australian Web Archive, including former AGWA/PANDORA** | Official archival recovery for disappeared government pages. NLA's [2017–18 report](https://pandora.nla.gov.au/partners/Annual_report17-18.pdf) evidenced government web captures from 1996; its [2018–19 report](https://www.pandora.nla.gov.au/partners/Annual_report18-19.pdf) records AGWA integration into Trove in 2019. [PANDORA overview](https://www.pandora.nla.gov.au/overview.html) describes full-text/URL search and selected-title catalogues. | [Public archive access](https://pandora.nla.gov.au/policy_practice.html) does not establish a reusable licence for every captured page. No open bulk-WARC export route was verified. Use only for named missing originals or versions: snapshot duplication, rewritten links, incomplete captures and capture/publication distinctions make broad harvesting costly. This web route cannot by itself supply 1988–1995 web observations. |

An actual 1988 PM record is [Hawke transcript 7370, released 7 August 1988](https://pmtranscripts.pmc.gov.au/release/transcript-7370), with two-page original PDF and readable OCR. This verifies one eligible original route, not the year's completeness. PM interviews and joint releases also contain journalists and other speakers: preserve each utterance's holder rather than assigning the whole page to the prime minister.

## Ready parliamentary data: useful shortcut, narrower actual span

The [RAPID-CDL deposit](https://zenodo.org/records/22868754), DOI **10.5281/zenodo.22868754**, version **2026-09-21**, is prepared by University of Queensland researchers from official parliamentary XML/SGML. Its [small README](https://zenodo.org/records/22868754/files/README.md?download=1) documents sitting-level ParlInfo/PDF links, paragraph order, procedural-unit type, and date-valid speaker details. It distinguishes speeches, written/spoken questions and answers, petitions, motions and table content. These are derived analytical tables, not a new independent source or seven million independent speeches.

The [machine-readable deposit metadata](https://zenodo.org/api/records/22868754) records publication date 2026-09-21 but creation on 2026-09-22; that distinction is retained. It specifies CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 and the following file sizes. Only that metadata, documentation and **HTTP 206 metadata footers** were inspected. No Parquet text rows were fetched and no complete Parquet file was downloaded.

| Verified item | Result and interpretation |
|---|---|
| Total deposited files | 502,442,451 bytes, including four Parquet tables and 6,514-byte README. This is compressed on-disk content, not the full computation budget. |
| `paragraph.parquet` | 500,382,872 bytes; **6,911,269 rows**, 57 row groups; schema includes IDs, sequence, speaker details, debate, procedural unit and **text**. |
| Encoded text-column sizes | 486,647,601 compressed bytes and 1,933,898,568 uncompressed encoded bytes. The latter is Parquet's encoded-column accounting, **not** measured plain UTF-8 size, tokens, sentences or embedding storage. |
| `session.parquet` | 101,818 bytes; **3,300 sittings**; non-null date bounds **1996-05-30–2026-09-08**. Both House and Senate occur in column statistics; chamber counts and archive completeness are unknown. Four PDF-link values are null. |
| `speaker_detail.parquet` | 17,150 bytes; 865 rows. Exported fields include Parliament identifier, names, gender, party and validity dates. There is **no exported ministerial-office or government/opposition status field**. |
| Provenance/coverage limit | The [project's creation instructions](https://github.com/rapid-community-data-lab/australian_federal_parliament/blob/main/datasets.md) describe an intended 1901-present fetch process. That must not override the deposited session-table bounds. The deposit does not establish 1988–1996 coverage, continuous monthly presence or observations through 21 September. |

Footer method: use the recorded exact file length; request the final eight bytes and the declared metadata footer with bounded Range requests; require HTTP 206 and exact lengths; parse only file metadata. The initial three-file inspection transferred 55,742 bytes of footer/trailer metadata. A second 1,241-byte session-footer check established chamber bounds and missing PDF-link counts. These statistics are direct metadata observations rather than author narrative claims.

A second available [Katz/Alexander deposit](https://zenodo.org/records/7799678), version v4, covers **House of Representatives 2 March 1998–8 September 2022**, derived from official XML, with 326.2 MB CSV ZIP or 467.1 MB Parquet ZIP. It is a fallback or cross-check, not another independent parliamentary stream and not Senate/full-period coverage. No archive was downloaded. Prefer the recent RAPID route when its lineage/unit/rights checks fit the chosen comparison.

Parliament is an institution-based Australian source frame, but its members include government, opposition and independents; petition text can represent other holders. Party plus sitting date is not sufficient to label ministerial responsibility. Add evidenced office and governing-coalition intervals where needed, preserve procedural context and quotation, and attribute expression to the recorded speaker. Institutional risk language is not automatically an institutional emotion or fear.

## Bounded recommendation and planning schedule

Start with **PM Transcripts for executive expression**, and **RAPID/official Hansard for a separately attributed parliamentary frame**. Select a recent period that overlaps usable Australian media/community evidence; keep older parliamentary/PM extensions supplementary unless a named historical comparison needs them. Do not collect all legislation, budget PDFs or web snapshots simply to fill the calendar.

Proposed next feasibility step, only under a new bounded scope: inspect 30–40 PM records across pre/post-1997 formats and the chosen contemporary window, and a small set of parliamentary sittings/utterances with both governing and opposition speakers. Retain fixed-cutoff filtering, source/native IDs, original URLs, release/sitting date, retrieval/version date, complete parent units and segment lineage. Reconcile OCR against named originals and distinguish petitions/tables/quoted speech. Measure bytes and segment multiplicity before approving a larger tranche. No climate/fear filter or model belongs to structural acquisition acceptance.

Planning estimates below are staff-work estimates, **not measured throughput or execution authorization**:

| Option | Initial feasibility | Subsequent bounded preparation/QA |
|---|---:|---:|
| Ready RAPID corpus, declared date/chamber view | 2–4 working days for schema, rights, unit and original-link checks; no collector construction needed | Approximately 1–2 weeks for an accepted derived view, dated role mapping and proportionate changed-tranche checks; historical gaps remain explicit |
| PM official XML route, declared source/time window | 3–5 working days to verify endpoint returns, inventory dates, OCR and native units | Approximately 2–4 weeks for a bounded extract/load and source/era QA; older OCR/PDF recovery may add time |

Downloading a 0.50 GB ready table is not the principal schedule risk. Source/date/role validation and later measure validation dominate; actual network, disk and model timings require the bounded benchmark. The new 50/100 GB Australian-analysis envelope must account for inputs, derived text/segments, vectors, indexes, checkpoints and **peak temporary** files; preserved international raw remains outside it. These source sizes alone cannot certify either budget. A ready paragraph table supplies an initial unit count, but the Australian date subset, chosen segmentation, overlap, title vectors, model dimension/precision and index design determine the final computation scale.
