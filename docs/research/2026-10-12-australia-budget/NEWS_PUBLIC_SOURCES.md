# Australian news and public-expression source options

Evidence checked: 12 October 2026. This is source/access research, not acquisition permission or an analytical corpus release. The empirical boundary is Australian institutions, publishers and evidenced Australian community context; individual historical author residence is not required or inferred. Eligible publication dates remain **1988-01-01–2026-09-21**, with September 2026 partial.

## Start with the saved material

The [accepted Australian projection](../2026-10-12-australia-scope/AUSTRALIA_CANDIDATE_FRAME.json) records **8,169 complete newspaper article IDs from nine publishers** and, separately, **45,679 dated community-context bodies**. These are candidate pools, not climate-relevant denominators or a population sample. International holdings and frozen counts remain preserved.

Read-only summaries of the existing [newspaper source-month metadata](../../../outputs/seminar/build_v14/figure_source_data/newspaper_final_source_month.csv) and [social source-month metadata](../../../outputs/seminar/build_v14/figure_source_data/social_final_source_month.csv) show:

|---|---:|---|---:|
| Green Left | 3,712 articles | 1991-02–2004-02 | 155 |
| Northern Rivers Times | 2,858 articles | 2024-06–2026-09 | 28 |
| Other seven Australian newspaper publishers | 1,599 articles | Separate source windows, 2013–2024 | Each occupies 1–15 months |
| Aus.Social | 35,821 dated bodies | 2020-04–2026-09 | 50 |
| Aussie.zone | 9,858 dated bodies | 2023-06–2026-09 | 40 |

Green Left and Northern Rivers Times comprise **80.4%** of the newspaper stock, but occupy different eras and editorial frames. The first–last span does not establish continuous availability. A concrete existing media/public overlap candidate is **2024-06–2026-09**, centred on Northern Rivers Times and the two communities; this remains a regional newspaper/community comparison, conditional on government evidence and structural checks. It cannot support a national three-role series from 1988.

## Six source families worth distinguishing

| Source/database | Actual era and native material | Access, licence and bulk readiness | Effort and useful feasibility question |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Trove digitised newspapers** | Broad historical holdings mostly precede the study. A documented exception is **Canberra Times, 1926–1995**, giving a potential 1988–1995 intersection. Archival newspaper reproduction with OCR and page images. | Public web access and a documented API; scaled use needs a key/terms check. Post-1954 access depends on negotiated publisher permission; public access is not a blanket reuse licence. NLA's 2026 note distinguishes copyright expiry from actual display availability. [NLA source](https://www.library.gov.au/sites/default/files/documents/2026-02/friends-newsletter-autumn-2026.pdf.pdf), [official API release](https://trove.nla.gov.au/sites/default/files/attachments/2023-07/Introducing%20Trove%20API%20v3%20-%20Release%203%20updated.pdf). | A named early event/title window is plausible. Allow several days for OCR/completeness and multi-page article mapping; broad issue recovery is substantially slower. Check article IDs, dates, readable complete bodies, existing overlap and applicable source terms first. |
| **ProQuest Australia & New Zealand Newsstream / Historical Australian Collection** | Newsstream provides text from newspapers, wires, websites, blogs and ABC broadcasts; publisher brochure describes backfiles to the **late 1990s**, varying by title. Historical collection advertises **The Age 1854–2000 / Sydney Morning Herald 1831–2000**, searchable at page level. [Newsstream provider catalogue](https://pq-static-content.proquest.com/collateral/media2/documents/newsresources-catalog.pdf), [historical provider brochure](https://about.proquest.com/globalassets/proquest/files/pdf-files/brochures/brochure-australiannewspaperscollection.pdf). | Subscription/library login, not an openly downloadable Australian database. Reading access does not establish automated mining or local retention permission. July 2026 terms permit eligible content mining inside **TDM Studio Workbench** and export of compliant derived data; they do not promise local full-text export. [Current provider terms](https://about.proquest.com/en/about/supplemental-terms-of-use-tdm-studio/). ABC remains broadcaster material; New Zealand sources stay outside the Australian view. | First verify the user's actual institutional subscriptions, title/date holdings, Australian content eligible for TDM, and permitted outputs. Working estimate: 1–2 days of checks if access already exists; otherwise an uncertain institutional/vendor delay. Page PDFs require article reconstruction. No account, purchase or contact was initiated. |
| **NOW / English-Corpora.org Australian-labelled sources** | Web news from **2010 onward**. Official source list includes Australian conventional titles alongside ABC, SBS, commentary, magazines and sports sites; differently spelled source names can refer to one publisher. [Provider source list](https://www.english-corpora.org/now/help/now-sources.asp). | Separate paid full-text-data licence; monthly packages and source metadata are documented. Crucially, the supplier removes **10 words per 200**, reports residual duplicates/boilerplate and acknowledges country errors, including inferred country assignments. It is not a complete-article archive or an automatically verified Australian frame. [Text/metadata limitations](https://www.corpusdata.org/limitations_now.asp), [download formats](https://www.corpusdata.org/downloads.asp), [purchase conditions](https://www.corpusdata.org/purchase.asp). | Lower collection-code effort, higher admission/verification burden. A supplier sample would need to establish exact AU-title/date selection, procurement package size, missing passages and reuse rights. Prefer original full bodies for fear-specific attribution. No purchase/download was made. |
| **Lancaster CASS “Australia 2015/2016” corpus** | A research author's description records **nearly 13,000 articles / 7.4 million words / 18 Australian newspapers**, **August 2015–July 2016**. Selection required “Australia”, “Australian” or “Australians”; it is not all Australian news. [Author's source description](https://theconversation.com/he-vs-she-in-australian-media-coverage-what-the-language-of-news-tells-us-about-gender-imbalance-67139). | The author links to a full-corpus download, but that legacy Lancaster landing page failed in this check. Current file access, format, article provenance and licence remain **unverified**. A public article's CC licence does not license the underlying newspaper corpus. | Potentially useful bounded mainstream comparison with low storage demand, subject to the actual archive and terms. Limit route/licence feasibility to half a day; do not build the thesis schedule around a presently unverified download. Its inherited keyword frame must remain explicit. |
| **AusNC collections now in LDaCA** | AusNC was transferred in **2023**, with separate collections rather than one coherent modern-news dataset. **ACE: 1986** (outside interval); **ICE-AUS: 1992–1995**, about 1 million words, mostly speech; **Australian Radio Talkback: 2004–2006**, 27 transcribed recordings. [Custodian history](https://www.ldaca.edu.au/news/posts/ausnc/), [collection descriptions](https://www.ldaca.edu.au/about/sample-collections/), [Macquarie ART deposit record](https://researchers.mq.edu.au/en/datasets/australian-radio-talkback-corpus-art/). | Text/transcripts exist, but access and terms are collection/file specific. ICE text terms and retained ART audio terms allow limited research downloads rather than an unrestricted open corpus. The new ART file landing page was inaccessible here; its current text-file licence needs direct verification. [ICE text terms](https://docs.ldaca.edu.au/licenses/ice-aus/all/v1/), [ART audio terms](https://www.ldaca.edu.au/resources/ldaca-resources/licenses/australian-radio-talkback/audio/v1/AusNC_Terms_of_Use_Australian_Radio_Talkback.pdf). | Small reference or distinct historical expression subframes, not replacements for longitudinal newspapers/forums. Allow 1–2 days to check available transcript files, dates, speaker/host/caller units and terms if a named comparison warrants them. Published date ranges differ slightly across descriptions; use item-level dates before selection. |
| **Aus.Social / Aussie.zone native public text** | Existing saved windows above are the usable evidence. A contemporaneous administrator announcement establishes Aus.Social existed by **October 2018**, but does not establish an intact archive back to that date. [Administrator announcement](https://medium.com/@mrshlee/aus-social-welcome-to-thundertoot-59d881ad573). Aussie.zone foundation date was not independently established here; saved presence starts June 2023. | Native Mastodon/Lemmy IDs, full public body fields and pagination are documented; actual anonymous access depends on instance configuration. Platform software licensing does not license posts. The accepted source registry records bounded local research retention for Aus.Social, with no verified open-content grant and redistribution unconfirmed. Current Aus.Social web read was robots-blocked; Aussie.zone returned 403. [Mastodon API](https://docs.joinmastodon.org/methods/timelines/), [Lemmy API documentation](https://join-lemmy.org/docs/contributors/04-api.html). | **Reuse stock first**, with thread/reply/version/remote-origin checks. No public bulk dump or complete retrospective inventory was verified. A future bounded source check could resolve local versus federated units, actual history and current access terms. New API documentation/version availability is not a collection release. |

The often suggested **ABC “A Million News Headlines”** is a small downloadable **two-column headline/date** dataset for 2003-02-19–2021-12-31, about 64 MB, labelled CC0 by its compiler. It lacks full bodies and stable article URLs and is broadcaster-derived; it cannot replace complete newspaper texts or passage evidence. [Compiler's dataset page](https://www.kaggle.com/therohk/million-headlines/data). It was inspected, not downloaded.

## Practical choices and workload

1. **Preferred: reuse, then analyse declared windows.** Allow roughly **3–5 working days** for a versioned Australian metadata view, named structural checks and observation-window design; then use a separately authorised bounded passage/embedding benchmark before setting production capacity. The 2024-06–2026-09 regional comparison is a concrete candidate, not a promised three-role result. Older Green Left material can remain a distinct historical newspaper analysis. Preserve neutral and low-affect records.
2. **One named mainstream supplement.** If the chosen question needs another newspaper in the same window, verify institutional ProQuest access/TDM eligibility first; alternatively check the small CASS legacy corpus for a distinct 2015–2016 comparison. Budget **1–2 days for feasibility**, with supplier/institutional delays separate. Choose one evidenced gap, not a source-count target. New retrieval needs a bounded release.
3. **Historical case study only if required.** Trove's Canberra Times intersection can support a selected 1988–1995 case; ICE/talkback are separate speech frames. Reserve **about one week for a bounded title/event tranche**, conditional on a successful small completeness/rights check. Full-period backfill would be a separate, materially larger undertaking.

These durations are planning estimates, not measured acquisition or model throughput and not a revision to the project's stage dates. A **50 GB total post-segmentation analytical budget** should be treated as a ceiling, not a target to fill; **100 GB** is a conditional alternative only if a measured sample and justified source window require it. A larger ceiling does not fix date gaps, source concentration, missing passages, account rights or local full-text export limits. Reserve raw/derived lineage, vectors/indexes and temporary write peaks in the coordinator's capacity model; no exact bytes-per-source or embedding multiplier were measured here.

No full corpus was downloaded, no source API collection or registration/contact occurred, and no corpus/model execution, raw-body audit, database write, semantic exclusion or acquisition successor was performed. Primary provider/custodian/author pages and accepted metadata were the evidence used; access failures remain explicit.
