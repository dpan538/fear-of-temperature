## Operational choices

Q is primary; S is examined separately. Proposed monthly descriptive CCF scan: −6 to +6 months; VAR candidate orders: 1–3, selected on training data by BIC subject to diagnostic/sample adequacy. Frequency-adjusted alternatives are fixed in calendar time before outcomes. Predictions use identical chronological holdout dates and controls. Missing bins are not zero; S can be zero for a completely observed bin with no relevant material, while Q is undefined. No interpolation across source discontinuities.

ITS uncertainty must address autocorrelation/heteroskedasticity where sample size supports it; robust standard errors do not fix non-stationarity. Prespecified directional/event test families use Holm correction. These are diagnostic and reporting provisions for the existing analysis, not additional substantive model deliverables. Null findings do not justify switching explanations.
